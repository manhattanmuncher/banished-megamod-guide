Happiness

Happy citizens work; unhappy ones idle (Citizens). Building pages list which kind of happiness a building gives and how far it reaches. Five kinds raise it; one, Detraction, lowers it.

How to use this: place homes inside the radius of one building of each kind (Spirit, Goods, Entertainment, Safety, Health), and outside the radius of Detraction buildings. Births and marriages also raise happiness; deaths, sickness, starvation and freezing lower it.
