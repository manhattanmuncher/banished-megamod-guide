Livestock and pastures

All 16 animals on the wiki: what they give while alive, what they give when slaughtered, and how much space they need.

  1. Get animals Livestock is bought from merchants at a Trading Post (Trading). Some starting conditions include one (Starting Conditions).
  2. Give them a pasture A pasture is a player-drawn area, like the [[b:buildings:pastures:pasture]]. Each animal lists the pasture area it needs.
  3. Collect products “Periodic” products come while the animal lives (for example Sheep: Wool every 2.75 months). Slaughter products come when herders cull animals.
