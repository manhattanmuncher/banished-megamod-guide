Toolbar map

MegaMod puts the same building in several toolbars. This map lists every top-level toolbar from the wiki's “Where to Find” paths, the menus inside it and example buildings, so you know where to click.

Tip: you don't need to learn the toolbars. Search a building here, open its page, and its Where to Find section gives the exact click path, often more than one.
