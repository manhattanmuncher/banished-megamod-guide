Storage

Every barn, cart and stockpile on the wiki lists what it stores (“Storage Categories”) and how much. Grouped by what they hold, biggest first.

Stockpiles hold raw materials per tile; barns hold food and goods. Build storage close to where things are made and used: shorter walks mean more work (Pathfinding). Set resource limits so one resource can't fill everything (Resource Limits).
