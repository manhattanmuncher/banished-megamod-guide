Homestead toolbar

Everything in Food Production → Homestead: 95 small farm buildings that make food and materials without big fields. Most need no seeds, and many need no inputs at all. Numbers come from each building's wiki page.

Quick picks
  • Food in any season, no seeds: a Patchwork or Crop patch. See the Season planner for your climate.
  • Many foods from one building: a Farmyard. Food variety keeps people healthy (Citizens).
  • Free and no inputs: hedgerows and Fruit and Nut plots.
  • Needs a supply chain: Home Grown patches (compost or fertilizer) and Fairy Garden patches (Fairy Dust).

Work time is per cycle (lower is faster). The wiki does not say how many items one cycle makes.
