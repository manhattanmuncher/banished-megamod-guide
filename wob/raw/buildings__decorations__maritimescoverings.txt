A set of Necora Maritimes decorative roof coverings and awnings, used to span gaps between buildings and dress streets. Includes flat (1x/2x) and high/arched pieces with in/out/left/right/corner/end variants, each in **Brown** and **Gray** finishes. All are free with no footprint.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Style ^ Pieces ^
| Flat covering | 1x In, 2x In/Out/Left/Right, Corner, End Left/Right |
| High covering | High 1x, High Center/Corner/End, High Arch 1x/2x/End |

Each piece comes in Brown and Gray (F-key colour).
