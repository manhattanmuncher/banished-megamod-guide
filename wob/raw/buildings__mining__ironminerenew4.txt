The Mine (Renew 4) is the fourth renewal upgrade in the Editor's Choice Iron Mine chain. It increases the production cap to 12,000 and can be upgraded further.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:ironminerenew3|Mine (Renew 3)]].
