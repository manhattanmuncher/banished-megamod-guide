A Colonial Charter Wild Gatherer, upgraded from the [[buildings:gathering:cc17ws|Wild Shepherd]]. Gatherers collect wild food within a 35-tile radius. Accessed by upgrading an existing Wild Shepherd rather than placing directly from the toolbar.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:gathering:cc17ws|Wild Shepherd]].

----

===== Produces =====

  * Various wild food (depends on available map resources)
