A DS Mods Stone Bridge tavern that sits on a stone bridge span. A Brewer ferments fruits, grains, vegetables, and honey into Ale and Mead, then serves them on-site to provide Entertainment happiness across a 30-tile radius. Press F when placing to cycle through six visual variants.
