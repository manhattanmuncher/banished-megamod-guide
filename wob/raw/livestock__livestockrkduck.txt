Ducks are small livestock raised at a Pasture. They lay Eggs frequently and yield Duck Meat, Feathers, and Fertilizer when slaughtered. Their small pasture footprint makes them very space-efficient.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Raised at a Pasture.
