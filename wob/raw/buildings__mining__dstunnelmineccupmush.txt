Converts a Tunnel Mine into a Mushroom Farm for all-weather production of mushrooms. 1–3 Farmers are employed to grow the mushrooms.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from any Tunnel Mine variant: [[buildings:mining:dstunnelminecc|Tunnel Mine Random]], [[buildings:mining:dstunnelmineccselect|Tunnel Mine Select]], [[buildings:mining:dstunnelmineccup|Tunnel Mine Upgrade (Random)]], or [[buildings:mining:dstunnelmineccselectup|Tunnel Mine Upgrade (Select)]].
