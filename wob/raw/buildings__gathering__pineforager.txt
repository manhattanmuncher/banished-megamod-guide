A Maritimes Pine Set forager's cache. Gatherers forage for wild foods within a 25-tile radius, collecting Apples, Maple Sap, Blueberries, and Cranberries from natural resources. Intended for use alongside the Maple Forestry Tower or Maple Forestry to ensure fruit trees are planted in the area.
