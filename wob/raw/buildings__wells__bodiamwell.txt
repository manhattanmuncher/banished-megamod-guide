Bodiam Castle wells that provide Safety happiness and fire suppression within a 20-tile radius. Features a small fire particle effect. Can be removed via the upgrade button. Five variants are available; the Well (variant 0) is a plain well with no nomad attraction, while the remaining four also attract nomad immigrants at a fixed group-size percentage.
