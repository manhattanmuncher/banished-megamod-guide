A RedKetchup Editor's Choice orchard zone. Farmers tend and harvest fruit from a drag-placed area with 2×2 tile tree spacing. The zone can be sized up to 225 tiles; additional workers are assigned automatically as the zone grows.

<wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/ectoolbar.png?nolink|Editor's Choice}}</wrap> **Editor's Choice** → **Food** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildorchard.png?nolink|Orchard}}</wrap> **Orchard**
