An EB Modular Herbalist working lodge. Herbalists collect wild herbs from within a 30-tile radius and dispense them to nearby settlers. Also provides basic healing to citizens. Typically paired with the [[buildings:gathering:ebmodherbalisthouse|Modular Herbalist House]].
