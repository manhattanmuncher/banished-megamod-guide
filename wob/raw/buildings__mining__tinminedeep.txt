The Dark Tin Ore Mine Shaft is an upgraded Tinmine that extracts Tin Ore deeper underground. It can also smelt Tin directly by consuming Furnace Fuel. When depleted it can be upgraded to the Deep Tin Ore Mine Shaft for unlimited production.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the Tinmine.

----
