A DS Mods Small Village weaving guild. Weavers spin raw fibres (Hemp Leaf, Flax, Cotton) into Canvas, Linen, and Cloth, weave Hemp Leaf into Gathering Baskets, and tailor Wool Coats, Canvas Coats, and Winter Coats from raw textiles.
