Decorative wooden fencing from the EB Simple Village (embx61) mod. Combine the straight, corner, end, and gate pieces to fence off areas. Comes in a standard **Wood Fence** set and a **Fence-Less** (ghosted, overlapping-placement) set, plus a matching **End Railing** edge piece.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Set ^ Pieces ^
| Wood Fence | Corner, End, Gate, Straight, Straight Long |
| Fence-Less (ghosted) | Corner, End, Gate, Straight, Straight Long |
| End Railing | edge railing piece |
