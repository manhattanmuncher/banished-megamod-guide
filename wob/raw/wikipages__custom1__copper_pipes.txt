===== Overview =====




Copper Pipes are a forged item crafted from metal in the Banished MegaMod. They are produced at a forge or smithy and stored in a Barn, serving purposes in trade, construction, or equipping citizens. At a trade value of 20, they are highly prized by traveling merchants.
