A Colonial Charter seeds and animals trading post. Must be placed on water. Merchants arrive by river boat offering seeds and livestock. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Three merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |
| Crop Seed Merchant | Crop seeds |
| Orchard Seed Merchant | Orchard seeds |

All merchants accept wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, textiles, and clothing as payment.
