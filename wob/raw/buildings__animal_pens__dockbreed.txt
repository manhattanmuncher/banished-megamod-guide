A Colonial Charter Dock Chicken Breeder. A Wetworker raises chickens on the docks, randomly producing Eggs, Chickens, and Feathers. Build cost includes a starter clutch of Eggs.

===== Produces =====

Production is random among the following:

  * [[WikiPages:food:egg|Egg]]
  * [[WikiPages:food:chicken|Chicken]]
  * [[WikiPages:textile:feathers_feather|Feathers]]

Egg appears most frequently in the random pool, followed by Feathers, with Chicken being the rarest of the three.
