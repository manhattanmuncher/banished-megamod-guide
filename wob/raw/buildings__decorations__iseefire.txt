The decorative lighting pieces from RedKetchup's I See Fire mod — free-standing and wall-mounted torches plus candles, used to light streets, walls and courtyards at night. They are free to place. (The mod's larger fire features — Real Huge Bonfire, Real Bonfire, Real Firecamp and Real Firepot — are happiness/gathering buildings documented on their own pages.)

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Real Torch** — a torch that stands on the ground.
  * **Real Wall Torch** — a wall-mounted torch, in 9 mounting positions (Pos 1–9) to sit at the right height on different walls.
  * **Candles on a Stone** — a small cluster of candles.
