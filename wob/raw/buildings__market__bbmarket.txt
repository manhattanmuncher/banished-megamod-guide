A general-purpose market from the Bed & Breakfast mod set. It stocks a wide variety of goods and distributes them to citizens within its radius, providing Goods happiness while stocked.

===== Stocks =====

  * Food (Grain, Fruit, Vegetables, Meat/Fish)
  * Firewood
  * Tools
  * Medicine
  * Clothing
  * Textiles
  * Crafted Items (Custom 0)
  * Forged Items (Custom 1)
  * Fabrics (Custom 2)
  * Precious Items (Custom 6)
  * Misc Items (Custom 7)
