A RedKetchup seeds and livestock trading post. Must be placed on water. Merchants arrive by river boat offering crop and orchard seeds and livestock. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Two merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |

All merchants accept wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, textiles, clothing, industrial fuel, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, raw materials, building supplies, precious goods, and miscellaneous items as payment.
