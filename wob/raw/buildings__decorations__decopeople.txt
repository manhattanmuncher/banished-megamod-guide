Decorative human figures from Kid's Deco People mod — ghosted, free-placing statues of villagers used to bring streets, markets and yards to life. They have no footprint and overlap freely, and come in several outfit sets.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Vanilla Deco People** — base-game-styled figures.
  * **First Deco People** and **Rural Deco People** — alternate figure sets.
  * **New Outfit Deco People** — additional clothing variants.
  * Individual boys, girls, men and women in assorted colours.
