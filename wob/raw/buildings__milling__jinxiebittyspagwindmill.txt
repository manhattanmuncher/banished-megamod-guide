The Bitty Windmill (Pasta Set) is a Jinxie's Bitty Pasta Set building where a miller grinds wheat or corn into flour. See also the [[buildings:milling:jinxiebittywindmill|Bitty Windmill]] from the Village Set, which grinds a wider variety of grains.
