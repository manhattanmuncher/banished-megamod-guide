A traditional arched Japanese wooden footbridge from the A8 Japanese mod. Drag to place across water; cost scales per tile. Available in four wood-stain colors.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Variant ^
| Japanese Wood Bridge (natural) |
| Japanese Wood Bridge (Brown) |
| Japanese Wood Bridge (Dark) |
| Japanese Wood Bridge (Red) |

All variants share the same per-tile cost (3 work, 4 Wood) and are placed by dragging from shore to shore.
