Chickens are small vanilla livestock raised at a Pasture. They lay Eggs periodically and yield Chicken meat, Feathers, and Fertilizer when slaughtered. The MegaMod adds Feathers and Fertilizer to the vanilla slaughter products.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Raised at a Pasture.
