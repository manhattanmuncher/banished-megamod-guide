====== Achievements ======
----

Achievements are in-game milestones that unlock as your town grows and accomplishes specific goals. They are tracked per save and can be viewed from the main menu.

> **Note:** Achievements are disabled whenever any mod is active, including MegaMod. They are a vanilla Banished feature only.
