The New Medieval Town (NMT) walkable bridge sets from the RK Editor's Choice Edition — masonry bridges that carry roads and settlers across rivers. Three finishes are offered (one stone, two brick styles), each laid in selectable spans so a crossing can be sized to the water it spans.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **NMT Stone Bridge** — stone-faced bridge.
  * **NMT Brick Bridge 1** and **NMT Brick Bridge 2** — two brick finishes.
  * Each style has a Start piece and length segments of **1, 2, 4 and 8** tiles.
