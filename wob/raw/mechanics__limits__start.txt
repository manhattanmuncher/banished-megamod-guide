====== Resource Limits ======
----

The resource limit tool sets a cap on how much of a given resource the town will stockpile. Once a limit is reached, workers who produce that resource stop working and act as general Laborers instead.

Limits are useful when one resource would otherwise crowd out others. For example, if logs are produced continuously they can fill a Stockpile and leave no room for firewood or coal — which could result in citizens freezing. Setting a log limit keeps space available for other resources without requiring additional storage.

Limits can be set globally via the resource limit tool, or per-building on any building that produces a resource (crop fields, wood cutters, etc.).

----

===== Notifications =====

The event log generates two automatic notifications related to limits:

  * **Storage full** — no more room to store goods
  * **Running low** — stored amount has dropped below 10% of the set limit

As the town grows, limits should be raised periodically to keep pace with population needs. A food limit set too low for the population size will cause starvation even when farms are productive.
