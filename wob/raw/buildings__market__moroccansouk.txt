A Moroccan-style marketplace. Distributes food, fuel, tools, clothing, and materials to nearby settlers and provides Goods happiness when stocked.

===== Stocks =====

  * Edible food (all types)
  * Fuel (firewood)
  * Tools
  * Health items
  * Clothing
  * Iron and Logs
  * Textiles
