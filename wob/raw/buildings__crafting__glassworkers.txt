The Glassworks is a Colonial Resources mod crafting building. A Glassmaker melts Sand using Coal, Charcoal, or Furnace Fuel as a heat source to produce Glass or Glassware. See also the [[buildings:crafting:ebglassworks|Glass Works (EB)]] for a similar building from a different mod.
