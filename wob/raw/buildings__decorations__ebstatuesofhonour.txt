Monumental memorial statues from the EB (embx61) Statues of Honour mod. Each comes as a standard built statue, a free "Ghosted" no-footprint version, and a "Nomad" version that attracts wandering nomads to the colony.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Statue ^ Size ^ Build Cost ^
| Jukov | 2 × 4 | 24 work, 6 Wood, 10 Stone, 4 Iron |
| Pojarski | 3 × 2 | 24 work, 6 Wood, 10 Stone, 4 Iron |

Each is also available as a free Ghosted version and a Nomad-attracting version.
