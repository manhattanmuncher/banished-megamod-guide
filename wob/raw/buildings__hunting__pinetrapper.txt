A Maritimes Pine Set trapper. Trappers harvest wildlife traps and dens within a 20-tile radius, collecting Fur and game meat from beaver lodges, nests, fox holes, rabbit holes, and traps. Works with all Maritimes tree foresters (pine and maple).
