===== Overview =====




Bronze Statue is a crafted item produced by skilled workers in the Banished MegaMod. It is made using raw materials and kept in a Barn, serving as finished goods for trade or as inputs for further production. At a trade value of 270, it is highly prized by traveling merchants.
