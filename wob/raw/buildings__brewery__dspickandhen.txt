Pick & Hen Tavern is a DS Mods combined brewery and alehouse. Brewers produce a wide variety of Ales, Strong Ales, Fruit Wines, Mead, and Mulled drinks from grains, fruit, honey, and herbs, then serve them on-site to provide Entertainment happiness across a 30-tile radius. The tavern can be enlarged with up to two [[buildings:brewery:dspickandhenext1|right-side]] and two [[buildings:brewery:dspickandhenext2|left-side]] extensions.
