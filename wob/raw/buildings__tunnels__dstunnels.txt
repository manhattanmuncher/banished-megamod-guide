DS Tunnels — roads that pass through cliffs and terrain obstacles. Drag to place; cost scales per tile. Available in stone and timber styles, each with fixed-length 1/2/4/8-tile span pieces.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Variant ^ Build Cost (per tile) ^
| Stone Tunnel (style 1) | 3 work, 4 [[WikiPages:stone:stone|Stone]] |
| Stone Tunnel (style 2) | 3 work, 4 [[WikiPages:stone:stone|Stone]] |
| Rough Log Tunnel | 3 work, 3 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |
| Log Tunnel | 3 work, 4 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |

Each is placed by dragging, with fixed 1/2/4/8-tile span pieces.
