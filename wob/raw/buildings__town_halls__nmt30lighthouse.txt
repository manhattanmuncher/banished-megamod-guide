A monumental stone lighthouse from the New Medieval Town (NMT) set, part of the RK Editor's Choice Edition. It is a large decorative landmark for coastlines and harbours, built from a substantial mix of lumber, stone, iron and copper. (Distinct from the separate [[buildings:nomads:newmedievallighthouse|Medieval Lighthouse]].)
