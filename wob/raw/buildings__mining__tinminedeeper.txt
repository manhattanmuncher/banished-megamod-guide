The Deep Tin Ore Mine Shaft is the final tier of the Tinmine upgrade chain, extracting Tin Ore with no production cap. It can also smelt Tin directly by consuming Furnace Fuel.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:tinminedeep|Dark Tin Ore Mine Shaft]].

----

===== Produces =====

  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:tin_ore|Tin Ore]]
  * [[WikiPages:custom1:tin|Tin]] (consumes [[WikiPages:custom3:furnace_fuel|Furnace Fuel]])
