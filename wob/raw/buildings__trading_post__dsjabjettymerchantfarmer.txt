A DS Jetty & Bridge farmer's trading post. Traders work here and seed and livestock merchants arrive by river boat. Must be placed on water.

See also: [[buildings:trading_post:dsjabjettymerchantfarmertex2|Jetty Farmer's Merchant (Tex2)]] for the alternate texture variant.

===== Merchant =====

Three seed and livestock merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Seed Merchant Co. Trader | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Trader | Livestock |
| Farm Goods Merchant | Livestock, crop seeds, and orchard seeds |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
