An EB Village quarry converted into a resort area. Provides Entertainment happiness and acts as a small food market. Built by upgrading a Village Rock Quarry via its in-building upgrade option.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Not directly buildable. Upgrade a Village Rock Quarry using the upgrade option in the building's dialog.

----

===== Stocks =====

  * Edible food (all types)
