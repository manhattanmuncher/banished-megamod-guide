Drift Wood Collector is a DS Mods Jetty & Bridge resource gathering building placed on water. Workers collect drifting wood from the water, cycling between Wood and Firewood outputs. It has a maximum production of 300 before the local drift is exhausted, but can be upgraded to extend its lifespan.
