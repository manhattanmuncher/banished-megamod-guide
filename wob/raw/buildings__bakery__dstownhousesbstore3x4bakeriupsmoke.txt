The upgraded form of the Bryggen Bakeri, with added smoke effects. Bakes breads, pastries, and other goods from flour and other ingredients.

----

===== Where to Find =====

This building is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:bakery:dstownhousesbstore3x4bakeri|Bryggen Bakeri]].
