A set of DS Jetty & Bridge spanning bridges that connect jetty platforms across water. Includes straight truss bridges, a diagonal truss bridge, and a rough drawbridge. Each comes in two texture styles (T1 / T2). Complements the [[buildings:bridges:dsjabjettybridge|Village Truss Bridge]] and [[buildings:bridges:dsjabjettybridge02|Village Drawbridge]].

----

===== Variants =====

^ Bridge ^ Size ^ Build Cost ^

| Large Truss Bridge | 2 × 6 | 55 work, 5 Wood, 5 Stone, 18 Lumber, 5 Iron Fittings |
| Diagonal Truss Bridge | 6 × 6 | 47 work, 5 Wood, 3 Stone, 13 Lumber, 5 Iron Fittings |
| Rough Drawbridge | 1 × 4 | 34 work, 4 Wood, 5 Lumber, 5 Iron Fittings, 1 Wagon Parts |

Each is available in Texture #1 and Texture #2 styles.
