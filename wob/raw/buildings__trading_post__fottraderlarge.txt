A Forest Trader large trading post. Traders work here and merchants arrive by river boat offering food, goods, and general wares. All merchants accept only textiles (furs) as payment.

===== Merchant =====

Three merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, minerals, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies, precious goods, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, alcohol, and minerals |

All merchants accept only textiles as payment.
