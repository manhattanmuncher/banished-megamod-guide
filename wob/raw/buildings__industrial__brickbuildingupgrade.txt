The Brickworks (Upgrade) is a Colonial Charter industrial building that replaces the Kiln Building with expanded brick production capability. It functions identically to the [[buildings:industrial:brickbuilding|Brickworks]] but is accessed by upgrading an existing Kiln Building rather than being placed from the toolbar.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the Kiln Building.
