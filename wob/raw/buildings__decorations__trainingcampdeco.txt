The decorative props that dress up RedKetchup's Training Camp — palisade walls and gates, archery targets, banners and flags, wooden practice dummies, weapon tables, horses and horse-water troughs. They are cosmetic pieces for building out a military training ground or fort. (The Training Camp's working buildings live in the Training Camp Main and Training Camp Storage sets.)

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Palisades** — wall sections, corners and the Palisade Main Gate.
  * **Archery Targets** — practice targets.
  * **Flags & Banners**
  * **Wood Dummies** — sparring/practice dummies.
  * **Weapon Tables** — racks and tables of weapons.
  * **Horses** — decorative horses.
  * **Water for Horses** — troughs / watering pieces.
