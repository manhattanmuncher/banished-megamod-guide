A Colonial Charter small dry goods supplier trading post. Must be placed on water. Merchants arrive by river boat offering textiles, clothing, tools, herbs, crafted goods, and other manufactured items. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

One merchant type may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Textiles Merchant | Textiles, clothing, tools, herbs, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, precious goods, miscellaneous items |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
