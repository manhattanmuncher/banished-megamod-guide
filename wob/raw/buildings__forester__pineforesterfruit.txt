A Maritimes orchard forestry tower. Foresters plant, care for, and harvest wood from maple, apple, and oak trees within a 27-tile radius. Planted trees spawn wild foods harvestable by the [[buildings:gathering:pineforager|Maple Forager's Cache]] and Pine Trapper.
