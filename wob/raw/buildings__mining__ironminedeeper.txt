The Deep Mine is the final upgrade in the Colonial Iron Mine chain. It offers unlimited production capacity, allowing the mine to keep extracting resources indefinitely. Miners can produce Iron Ore, Coal, or smelt Iron using Furnace Fuel.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:ironminedeep|Dark Mine]].
