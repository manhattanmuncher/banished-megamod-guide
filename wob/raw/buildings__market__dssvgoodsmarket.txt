A large goods market from the DS Small Village mod. Distributes fuel, tools, clothing, building materials, and crafted goods to nearby settlers. Provides Goods happiness when a worker is assigned.

===== Stocks =====

  * Fuel and Firewood
  * Tools and Clothing
  * Textiles
  * Iron and Stone
  * Coal and Coke
  * Crafted Items (Custom 0)
  * Forged Items (Custom 1)
  * Fabrics (Custom 2)
  * Industrial goods / Furnace Fuel (Custom 3)
  * Materials (Custom 4)
  * Construction materials (Custom 5)
