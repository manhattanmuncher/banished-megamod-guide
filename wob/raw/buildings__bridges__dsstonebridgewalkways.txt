A set of DS Stone Bridge walkway pieces, used to build stone bridges and raised causeways over water. Combine straight sections, ramps, stairs, corners, 3-way and 4-way junctions, and a drawbridge. Pairs with the [[buildings:bridges:dsnormalstonebridgel|Stone Bridge Left/Right]] halves.
