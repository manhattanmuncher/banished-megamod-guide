A wrought-metal fence set from the RK Editor's Choice Edition — the standalone version of the railings used on the Metal Fence Cemetery, for enclosing gardens, plots and yards. Each piece costs two stone. Found on the same Creepy Cemetery toolbar as the [[buildings:cemeteries:rkmetalcemetery|Metal Fence Cemetery]].

----

===== Variants =====

  * Straight, Long Straight
  * Corner, End
  * 3-Way, 4-Way
  * Gate, Small Opening
  * Diagonal, Soft Turn Left, Soft Turn Right
