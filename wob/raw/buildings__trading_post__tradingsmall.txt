A Colonial Charter small farm supplier trading post. Must be placed on water. Merchants arrive by river boat offering seeds and livestock. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Two merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Seeds Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
