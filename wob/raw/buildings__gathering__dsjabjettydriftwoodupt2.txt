Drift Wood Collector (UP, Tex2) is the Texture #2 variant of the [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodup|Drift Wood Collector (UP)]]. It is the wind-down state of an exhausted Tex2-set Drift Wood Collector or Searcher: a single worker dismantles the workplace while gathering the last 50 units of driftwood (cycling between Wood and Firewood). Stats are identical to the UP variant; only the visual mesh differs.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Not available from the toolbar. Accessed via the **Upgrade** button on an exhausted [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollectort2|Drift Wood Collector (Tex2)]].

----

===== Produces =====

Production is random among the following:

  * [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]]
  * [[WikiPages:fuel:firewood|Firewood]]
