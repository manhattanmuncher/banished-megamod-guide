An Out of This World trading post. The General Merchant arrives by river boat and offers a wide variety of goods. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

One merchant type may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| General Merchant | Tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, precious goods, miscellaneous items, and crop and orchard seeds |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
