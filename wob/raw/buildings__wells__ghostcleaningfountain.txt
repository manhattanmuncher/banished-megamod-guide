A ghost version of the Colonial Charter Cleaning Fountain that provides Safety happiness and fire suppression within a 20-tile radius. Free to place with no build cost. As a ghost building, it has a 0×0 footprint and can be placed literally anywhere on the map. It cannot be removed with the standard removal tool — use the building's own interface to remove it. Shares the same appearance as the [[buildings:wells:cleaningfountain|Cleaning Fountain (Colonial Charter)]].
