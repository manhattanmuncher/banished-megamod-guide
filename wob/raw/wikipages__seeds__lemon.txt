Lemon Seeds are used to plant a Lemon tree in an [[buildings:orchards:start|Orchard]]. Farmers harvest [[WikiPages:food:lemon|Lemon]] using an Axe. Fruit cycles take 4 months; trees reach maturity in 24 months.

----

===== Procuring =====

Can be purchased from merchants at any [[buildings:trading_post:start|Trading Post]].
