A Colonial Charter stone house with upgrade paths to country house variants; houses up to 5 settlers.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Available on any Colonial Charter map as a CC-modified stone house. Can be upgraded to:
  * [[buildings:houses:stonehouseupgrade|Stone Home]]
  * [[buildings:houses:countryhouse3upgrade|Country House 3 (Upgrade)]]
  * [[buildings:houses:countryhouse4|Country House 4]]
  * [[buildings:houses:countryhouse5|Country House 5]]
