Bears are large livestock raised at a Pasture. They yield Bear Meat and Fur when slaughtered. Not available as an initial livestock option at game start — purchase breeding stock from a Trading Post to begin raising them.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Raised at a Pasture. Not offered as a starting livestock choice; must be acquired later via a Trading Post.
