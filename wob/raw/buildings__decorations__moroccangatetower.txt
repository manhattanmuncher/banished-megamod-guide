A 1×1 corner tower piece from the A8 Moroccan Misc mod, used to anchor the ends of wall segments. Three appearance variants are available. Part of the Moroccan Gate set along with the [[buildings:decorations:moroccangate|Moroccan Gate]], [[buildings:decorations:moroccangatedia|Moroccan Gate Diagonal]], and [[buildings:decorations:moroccangatewall|Moroccan Gate Wall]].
