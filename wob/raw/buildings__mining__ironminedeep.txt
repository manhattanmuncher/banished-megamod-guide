The Dark Mine is a deep upgrade of the Iron Mine, allowing continued resource extraction after the original mine nears depletion. Miners can choose to produce Iron Ore, Coal, or smelt Iron directly using Furnace Fuel.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:ironmine|Iron Mine]].
