====== Terrain ======
----

Terrain determines the shape of the map: how flat or hilly the land is, how many rivers and lakes appear, and the visual look of the ground. It is chosen when starting a new game and cannot be changed afterward. Terrain has no effect on climate, crop growth, or tornado frequency — those are controlled by the [[climate:start|Climate]] selection.

The MegaMod adds a large number of terrain types from Colonial Charter, Discrepancy, RK Editor Choice, CTP, and the Peaks Valley mod, in addition to the five vanilla types.

----

===== Vanilla Terrains =====

^ Terrain ^ Notes ^
| Valleys | Default starting terrain; gentle slopes, rivers, moderate lakes |
| Mountains | Rugged terrain with fewer flat building areas |

===== Notes =====

  * **[CC]** = Colonial Charter terrain. **[RKEC]** = RK Editor Choice terrain. **DS** terrains have no prefix tag in-game.
  * **Hidden terrains**: Four RKEC grid variants (Peaks Valley [Grid], Peaks Plain [Grid], Peaks Mountains [Grid], Peaks Lakes [Grid]) and two Peaks Valley grid variants (Peak's Plain (Grid), Peak's Valley (Grid)) exist in the mod files but have ''_exposed = false'' and do not appear in the new game menu.
  * **RKEC visual themes**: RK Editor Choice terrains are primarily texture reskins of the four vanilla base shapes. Gameplay behavior (flatness, rivers, lakes) depends on the base shape, not the visual theme.
  * **DS terrain naming**: The DS "Red Desert" and vanilla "Red Desert" are separate terrain generators. The DS version has a unique visual skin.
