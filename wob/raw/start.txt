===== What is Banished? =====

Banished is a city-building strategy game by Shining Rock Software where you guide a group of exiled travelers building a new life in the wilderness. There are no enemies — just weather, starvation, and an ageing population. Every decision about where you build, what you grow, and how you balance resources matters.
</WRAP>
<WRAP half column>
===== What is MegaMod? =====

MegaMod is a large compilation mod that bundles hundreds of community-created buildings, resources, and systems into a single compatible package. It greatly expands what Bannies can produce, trade, and consume — adding new food types, industries, themed building sets, and much more.
</WRAP>
