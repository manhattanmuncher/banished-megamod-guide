Drift Wood Searcher (Tex2) is an alternate texture variant of the [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollectorslct|Drift Wood Searcher]]. It has identical stats and produces the same outputs, but uses the Texture #2 visual style for the Jetty & Bridge mod set.
