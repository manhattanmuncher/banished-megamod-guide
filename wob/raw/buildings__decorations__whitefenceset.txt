RedKetchup's White Fence Set — a tidy white picket fence used as decoration or to mark out paths and garden edges. Each piece costs a single log, so a whole run is nearly free to build.

----

===== Variants =====

  * Straight, Straight Long
  * Corner, End
  * 3-Way, 4-Way
  * Door (gate), Trellis Archway
