===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^ Accepts ^
| Food Merchant | Food | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, herbs, food, textiles, clothing, alcohol |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol | Same |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, herbs, food, textiles, clothing, alcohol; crop and orchard seeds | Same |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, textiles, clothing |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, tools, textiles, clothing |
