The Quarry (Renew) is an upgrade of the base Quarry that restores it to production with an increased output cap of 3000. It can be upgraded further along the Renew chain for higher capacities.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:quarry|Quarry]].
