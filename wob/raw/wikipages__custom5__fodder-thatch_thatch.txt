===== Overview =====




Fodder-Thatch is a construction material used to erect buildings in the Banished MegaMod. It is produced or refined and held in a Stockpile, ready for builders to draw on when constructing new structures. Its trade value is low at 2, but it plays an important role in the colony's production chains.
