A set of decorated festival parks from Jinxie's Festival Park Set. Each is a free 5×5 plot that provides Entertainment happiness to citizens within a 20-tile radius, with 3 F-key variations for wood colour.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Park ^
| Bitty Village Park |
| Christmas Celebration in the Park |
| Easter Celebration in the Park |
| Halloween Celebration in the Park |
| Thanksgiving Celebration in the Park |
| Wedding Celebration in the Park |

All are free, 5×5, Entertainment happiness (radius 20), with 3 F-key wood-colour variants.
