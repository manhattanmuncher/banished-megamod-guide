A DS Thadd Surrel large industry trading post. Up to 34 traders work here, and industry merchants arrive by river boat offering raw materials, crafted goods, and construction supplies. No food merchants visit this post.

===== Merchant =====

Multiple industry merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Thadd Surrel Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, coal, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies, and misc |
| Resources | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, and industrial fuel |
| TS Materials | Coal, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies |
| DS Trading Co. | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, coal, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies, and misc |
| Goods Trading Co. | Crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, textiles, tools, and clothing |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
