A Kid - Nomads decorative well that attracts nomad immigrants. Each variant limits the maximum size of arriving nomad groups as a percentage of total population. Free to place, max 1 instance per variant. Seven variants are available — the plain "Nomad Well" variant is purely decorative with no immigration effect (its immigration behavior is not active in the underlying data).
