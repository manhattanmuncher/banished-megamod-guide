New Styth Tower is a DS Mods watchtower that grants Safety happiness over a wide radius. When staffed by Village Guards, the tower confiscates a varied supply of goods from bandits in the surrounding area — food, tools, alcohol, clothing, fuel, herbs, and precious materials — and delivers them to the town's stockpiles. Only one New Styth Tower can be built per map.
