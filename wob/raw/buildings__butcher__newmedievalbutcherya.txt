An upgraded Medieval Butchery with improved efficiency.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Butchery (Upgrade A) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:butcher:newmedievalbutchery|Medieval Butchery]].
