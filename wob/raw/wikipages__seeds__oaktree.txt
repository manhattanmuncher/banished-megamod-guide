Oak Tree Seeds are used to plant an Oak tree in an [[buildings:orchards:start|Orchard]]. Farmers harvest [[WikiPages:food:acorns|Acorns]] using an Axe. Fruit cycles take 5 months; trees reach maturity in 56 months.

----

===== Procuring =====

Can be purchased from merchants at any [[buildings:trading_post:start|Trading Post]].
