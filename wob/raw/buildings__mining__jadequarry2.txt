The Jade Quarry 2 is an upgraded Jade Quarry with a faster work cycle. It maintains the same 1000-unit cap but completes each cycle more quickly. When depleted, it can be upgraded to the final tier for unlimited production.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:jadequarry|Jade Quarry]].
