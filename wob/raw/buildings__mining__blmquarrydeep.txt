A Deep Surface Mine for mining Stone and Salt. Provides much greater resource production. Upgraded versions allow the ground to be reclaimed on removal.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:blmquarry|Stone & Salt Mine]].
