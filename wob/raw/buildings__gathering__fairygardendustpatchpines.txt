A Fairy Garden dust plot with a pines roof. Fairies tend the plot to gather Fairy Dust, which powers the Fairy Garden patches. This is the pines roof variant.

See also: [[buildings:gathering:fairygardendustpatch|Fairy Dust Plot (Seasonal)]], [[buildings:gathering:fairygardendustpatchleaves|Fairy Dust Plot (Leaves)]]
