Compact footbridges from Jinxie's Bitty Village Set, used to cross streams and rivers. Drag to place over water; cost scales per tile. Available in three appearance styles.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Variant ^ Toolbar ^
| Bitty Wood Style Bridge | Bridges → Wood Bridges |
| Bitty Stone Style Bridge | Bridges → Stone Bridges |
| Bitty Slate Style Bridge | Bridges → Slate Bridges |

All share the same per-tile cost (3 work, 2 Wood).
