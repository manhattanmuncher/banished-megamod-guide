A decorative Moroccan gate from the A8 Moroccan Misc mod. The center two tiles of the 6×1 footprint are passable, forming a walkable gate arch. Three appearance variants are available. Pairs with the [[buildings:decorations:moroccangatetower|Moroccan Gate Tower]] and [[buildings:decorations:moroccangatewall|Moroccan Gate Wall]] pieces.
