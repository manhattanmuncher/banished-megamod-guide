The Precious Mine is a Colonial Charter mine that extracts Silver, Gold, and Gems from rich ore deposits. Miners produce one of the three materials randomly each cycle. The mine has a limited total yield of 1000 units and creates a happiness detraction for nearby citizens. It can be upgraded twice to improve efficiency.
