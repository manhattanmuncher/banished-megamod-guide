Decorative crop tiles from the New Medieval Town (NMT) set — purely cosmetic plots of vegetables and grains for dressing up gardens, market stalls and farm scenery. They grow nothing and are free to place; they just add the look of a planted crop.

----

===== Variants =====

  * Barley, Sorghum
  * Watermelon, Tomato, Strawberry
  * Broccoli, Lettuce, Kale, Spinach
  * Carrot, Cucumber, Pea, Radish
