A Maritimes pine forestry tower. Foresters plant, care for, and harvest wood from fir, spruce, and white pine trees within a 27-tile radius. Planted trees spawn resources harvestable by the [[buildings:gathering:pineforager|Maple Forager's Cache]] and Pine Trapper.
