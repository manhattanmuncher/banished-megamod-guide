The Marble Quarry 2 is an upgraded Marble Quarry with a faster work cycle. It maintains the same 1000-unit cap but completes each cycle more quickly. When depleted, it can be upgraded to the final tier for unlimited production.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:marblequarry|Marble Quarry]].
