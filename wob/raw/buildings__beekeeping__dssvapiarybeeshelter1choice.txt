A DS Mods Small Village bee shelter that lets the player choose which output to produce: Honey, Comb Honey, Beeswax, or Royal Jelly. Identical stats to the random-output [[buildings:beekeeping:dssvapiarybeeshelter1|Village Bee Shelter]].
