====== Disasters ======
----

Disasters are random events that threaten your settlement. The game includes three types: disease outbreaks, fires, and tornadoes. Disease is the most common threat; fires and tornadoes are rarer but can destroy buildings and kill citizens instantly.

----

===== Disease =====

Disease outbreaks occur randomly. When an outbreak starts, infected citizens spread the disease to nearby healthy citizens within a radius every quarter year. The [[buildings:hospital:doctorhouse|Doctor's House]] reduces disease spread and death rates; [[buildings:gathering:herbalist|Herbalist]] provides a passive health boost that helps citizens recover. The [[professions:start|Doctor]] profession is immune to disease and will not die from it.

**Infection %** — chance a healthy citizen within range catches the disease each transmission cycle. **Fatality** — once infected, 1 in N citizens will die. **Radius** — tile range for person-to-person spread.

^ Disease ^ Infection % ^ Radius ^ Fatality (1 in N) ^ Notes ^
| Influenza | 100% | 8 | 1 in 50 | Spreads to almost everyone but rarely kills |
| Mumps | 90% | 8 | 1 in 35 | Very contagious; low death rate |
| Diphtheria | 80% | 8 | 1 in 80 | Very contagious; lowest death rate of all diseases |
| Dysentery | 80% | 8 | 1 in 25 | Very contagious; moderate death rate |
| Yellow Fever | 60% | 8 | 1 in 20 | Moderately contagious; notable fatality |
| Scarlet Fever | 50% | 8 | 1 in 15 | Even spread and death chance |
| Typhus | 40% | 8 | 1 in 12 | Moderately contagious; significant death rate |
| Measles | 30% | 8 | 1 in 10 | Less common; 10% fatality |
| Tuberculosis | 20% | 8 | 1 in 8 | Uncommon; ~12.5% fatality |
| Small Pox | 10% | 8 | 1 in 5 | Rare outbreak; 20% fatality |
| Cholera | 5% | 12 | 1 in 4 | Rare but very lethal; wider spread radius |
| Plague | 1% | 12 | 1 in 3 | Very rare; highest death rate; wider spread radius |

----

===== Fire =====

Buildings can spontaneously catch fire or ignite from a neighboring burning structure. Once a fire starts, it spreads to adjacent buildings approximately once per in-game day. Citizens automatically fight fires by carrying water; up to 50 workers can respond at once. A fire requires 100 units of water to extinguish and burns for approximately one month if not put out.

Each building is assigned one of five fire types, which determine its spontaneous ignition risk and how readily it spreads fire to neighbors. Buildings with **ignition years = 0** never start fires on their own — they can only catch fire from a burning neighbor.

**Ignition** — average years between spontaneous fires at a population of 100 (fires become more frequent as population grows). **Spread** — chance per spread interval that the fire jumps to an adjacent building.

^ Fire Type ^ Typical Buildings ^ Ignition (yrs @ pop 100) ^ Spread Chance ^ Notes ^
| Wood House | Wood residential buildings | 20 years | 50% | Standard ignition risk |
| Wood Building | Wood non-residential buildings | Never | 50% | Can only catch fire from neighbors |
| Stone House | Stone residential buildings | 30 years | 33% | Harder to ignite; spreads less |
| Stone Building | Stone non-residential buildings | Never | 33% | Can only catch fire; spreads less |
| Production | Workshops and production buildings | 40 years | 50% | Least likely to self-ignite |
| Dock House | Dock buildings | 11 years | 80% | High ignition risk; spreads aggressively |
| Lumber Mill (DS) | DS Lumber Mill | 5 years | 80% | Highest ignition risk in the game |

Stone buildings are meaningfully safer — they ignite less often and spread fire at 33% versus 50% for wood. Dock buildings and the DS Lumber Mill are the greatest fire hazards and should not be placed adjacent to dense housing.

----

===== Tornadoes =====

A tornado touches down near the town, carving a path through buildings and killing any citizens caught in its way. There is no warning and no counter-measure — buildings in the tornado's path are destroyed instantly. The only mitigation is climate selection at the start of a new game.

**Path and destruction:** The tornado travels at 3 meters per second and covers a minimum of 64 tiles. It destroys everything within 8 tiles of its center line, with additional random destruction reaching up to 20 tiles from that line. A single tornado can cut a wide, unpredictable swath through a densely built settlement.

**Frequency:** Tornado risk is controlled entirely by the chosen climate. Each climate defines an average years-between-events at low population; larger towns attract tornadoes more frequently as the population component of the formula grows. See the [[climate:start|Climate]] page for per-climate tornado frequencies.

Climates with notably different tornado risk:

  * **Never**: Very Mild (99,999 years — effectively impossible)
  * **Extremely rare**: Marine (~1,000 years), DS Temperate (~889 years)
  * **Very rare**: Tropical, Warm Arid, Harsh Arid (~600 years each)
  * **Occasional**: Most climates (~100 years at low population)
  * **Frequent**: DS Harsh Cold (~55 years), Very Harsh (~33 years)
