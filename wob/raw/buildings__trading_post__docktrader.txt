===== Merchant =====

Multiple merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| General | All good categories, crop and orchard seeds, and livestock |
| Organics | Food items (fruit, vegetables, grain, and protein) |
| Resources | Crafted goods, textiles, clothing, tools, herbs, alcohol, forged goods, fabric, precious, and misc |
| Resources | Raw materials (wood, stone, iron, fuel, coal, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies) |
| Seeds | Crop and orchard seeds, and livestock |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
