A Jinxie's Bitty Village Set seed and livestock trading post. Merchants arrive by river boat offering crop seeds, orchard seeds, and livestock. Can be upgraded to the [[buildings:trading_post:jinxiebittytrader|Bitty Trader]] for access to all merchant types. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.
