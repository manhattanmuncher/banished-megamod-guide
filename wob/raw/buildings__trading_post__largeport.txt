A KID mod massive river trading port. Merchants arrive by river boat offering all resource categories. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies |
| General Merchant | Tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, precious goods, and miscellaneous items |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
