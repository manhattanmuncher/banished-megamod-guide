===== Overview =====

The Mill is a food processing building from the More Havens mod in the Banished MegaMod. A Miller grinds grain into Flour, accepting Wheat, Corn, or Oats as inputs.

----

===== Toolbar =====

This building's toolbar registration is disabled in the current mod version and does not appear in the build menu.
