A DS Thompson food trading post. Traders work here and food merchants arrive by river boat offering grain, vegetables, and fruit. All merchants exclusively offer food crops.

===== Merchant =====

Seven food merchant types may visit by river boat, all specializing in grain, vegetables, and fruit:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Thompson Grain Trader | Grain |
| Fresh Vegetable Trader | Vegetables |
| merchant from Fruits Of All Seasons | Fruit |
| Grains, Fruits and Veg merchant | Grain, fruit, and vegetables |
| merchant of Brent's Veggies | Vegetables |
| Dry Grains merchant | Grain |
| Thompson Fresh Trader | Grain, fruit, and vegetables |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
