A decorative flower-bed tiling system from Kid's Ghost Flowers mod — coloured flowers laid as tile pieces (center, corner, edge and row sections) so a flower bed of any shape can be filled in. Purely cosmetic and free to place, in a normal and a "High" (taller) style.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Tile pieces** — Center, Center Corner, Center Row, Corner, Edge, Edge Corner, Edge Row.
  * **Bed styles** — base bed (B) and second bed (B2), each in a normal and a **High** height.
  * **Colours** — multiple flower colours (Blue and others).
