===== Overview =====




Coins are a precious item valued for their rarity or beauty in the Banished MegaMod. They are stored in a Barn and function primarily as luxury trade goods, attracting favourable prices from merchants. Their trade value is low at 1, but they play an important role in the colony's production chains.
