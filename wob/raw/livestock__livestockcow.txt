Beef Cows are vanilla Cattle raised at a Pasture. They yield Beef and Leather when slaughtered. The MegaMod adds a second Beef drop, Fertilizer, and Mountain Oyster to the vanilla slaughter products.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Raised at a Pasture.
