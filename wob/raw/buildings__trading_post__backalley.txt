A Colonial Charter back-alley **Bootlegger** (template ''BackAlley''). It provides all manner of bootlegged items — for a price in [[WikiPages:custom6:silver_pfennig|Silver Pfennig]]. The worker turns silver coins into a wide range of contraband goods.
