The Tool Maker is the vanilla Banished tool production building. Tool Makers craft iron tools and steel tools for the colony's workers, improving their productivity.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Tool Maker is found in the vanilla Banished **Resource Production** building category.

<wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/toolbarrefined.png?nolink|Refined Resources}}</wrap> **Refined Resources** -> <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/toolbarmetalworking.png?nolink|Tools and Forge}}</wrap> **Tools and Forge** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildtoolmaker.png?nolink|Tool Maker}}</wrap> **Tool Maker**
