An upgraded medieval tailor with a brick exterior. Makes clothes for your citizens.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Tailor (Upgrade 2) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:clothing:newmedievaltailorupg1|Medieval Tailor (Upgrade 1)]].
