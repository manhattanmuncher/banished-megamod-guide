A Colonial Charter Pearl Stand where a dredger processes oysters to occasionally find a rare pearl. Most cycles return oysters; one in twenty-eight yields a pearl.

===== Produces =====

Each work cycle consumes 10 [[WikiPages:food:oyster|Oyster]]. Production is random:
  * [[WikiPages:food:oyster|Oyster]] (27 out of 28 cycles)
  * [[WikiPages:custom6:pearl|Pearl]] (1 out of 28 cycles)
