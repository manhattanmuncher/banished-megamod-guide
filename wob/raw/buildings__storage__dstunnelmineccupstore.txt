A DS Mods large underground food cellar built into an old mine. Settlers store food, fruit, vegetables, grain, and protein with a capacity of 20,000 units.

----

===== Where to Find =====

This building is not built directly — it is the upgraded form of the Tunnel Mine CC. Use the upgrade option on the Tunnel Mine CC building to convert it to an Old Mine Food Cellar.
