A set of DS Celtic House defensive timber palisade pieces. Combine straight, corner, open, gate, and tower pieces to fortify a settlement. Available in two heights — low (05) and high (10).

----

===== Variants =====

^ Piece ^ Size ^ Build Cost ^
| Straight (1×1) | 1 × 1 | 5 work, 4 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |
| Corner | 2 × 2 | low Wood |
| Open / Water | 1 × 1 | low Wood |
| Gate | 4 × 1 | 21 work, 13 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |
| Tower | 4 × 4 | 34 work, 21 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |

Each piece comes in low (05) and high (10) height variants.
