A Necora invisible utility square that provides Safety happiness and fire suppression without a visible building, letting you cover an area with fire protection discreetly. Three variants are included.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Variant ^
| An Empty Square (blocks movement) |
| A Passable Square (walkable) |
| An Empty Field (drag-to-size zone) |

All are free to place.
