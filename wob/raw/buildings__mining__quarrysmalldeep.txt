The Deep Small Quarry is an upgraded Small Quarry that digs deeper for more Stone. When depleted it can be upgraded to the Deeper Small Quarry for unlimited production.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:quarrysmall|Small Quarry]].
