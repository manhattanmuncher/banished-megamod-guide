A Colonial Charter Distillery where a Distiller bottles grains, fruit, herbs, and other organics into Spirits or Aqua Vitae. Spirits are bottled in Glassware; Aqua Vitae uses Canteens. Every recipe also burns Furnace Fuel.

----
