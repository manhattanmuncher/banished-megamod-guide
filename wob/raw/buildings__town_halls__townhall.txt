The Town Hall is the vanilla Banished administrative building, required for the colony to grow beyond a certain population. In vanilla, Nomads only begin arriving once the colony reaches a population of 8; MegaMod removes this threshold, so Nomads can arrive from the start.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Town Hall is found in the vanilla Banished **Social** building category.

<wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/toolbarnomad.png?nolink|Nomad}}</wrap> **Nomad** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildtownhall.png?nolink|Town Hall}}</wrap> **Town Hall**
