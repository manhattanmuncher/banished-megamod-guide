A Christmas Mod gingerbread house (honey variant) providing Spirit happiness within a radius of 35 tiles. Also provides resource limit controls for the colony. Only one can be placed per colony. Uses Honey instead of Sugar (Refined) compared to the standard [[buildings:town_halls:gingerbreadhouse|Gingerbread House]].
