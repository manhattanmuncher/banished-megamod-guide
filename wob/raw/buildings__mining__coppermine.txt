The Copper Ore Mine Shaft is a Colonial Charter mine that extracts copper ore from the ground. Miners can work it as an ore excavation or smelt copper directly using Furnace Fuel. The mine has a limited total yield of 350 units before it depletes, after which it can be upgraded to a Dark Copper Ore Mine Shaft for continued production.
