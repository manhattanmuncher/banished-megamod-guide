===== Overview =====




Peg Leg is a crafted item produced by skilled workers in the Banished MegaMod. It is made using raw materials and kept in a Barn, serving as finished goods for trade or as inputs for further production. It carries a trade value of 8, making it a worthwhile commodity for trade.
