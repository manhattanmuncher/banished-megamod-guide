A large set of decorative EB (embx61) wooden fence pieces, available in four wood finishes — **Cherry, Oak, Wenge, and White**. Each finish includes straight, corner, end, gate, 3-way, and 4-way pieces, plus capped (C) and ghosted sub-variants, for building fully connected fences around gardens and properties.
