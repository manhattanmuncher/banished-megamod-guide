===== Overview =====




Tinware is a forged item crafted from metal in the Banished MegaMod. It is produced at a forge or smithy and stored in a Barn, serving purposes in trade, construction, or equipping citizens. It carries a trade value of 10, making it a worthwhile commodity for trade.
