Drift Wood Collector (UP) is the wind-down state of an exhausted Drift Wood Collector. After the parent collector reaches its production limit, the upgrade button on the building dismantles the workplace while a single worker gathers the last 50 units of driftwood (cycling between Wood and Firewood) before the structure is removed.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Not available from the toolbar. Accessed via the **Upgrade** button on an exhausted [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollector|Drift Wood Collector]], [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollectorslct|Drift Wood Searcher]], or [[buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollectorslctt2|Drift Wood Searcher (Tex2)]].

----

  * [[WikiPages:fuel:firewood|Firewood]]
