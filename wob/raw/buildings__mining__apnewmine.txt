The New Mine is an Abandoned Places building that gives miners a choice of Iron, Coal, or Gold depending on the local terrain.

----

===== Where to Find =====

<wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/kidtoolbar.png?nolink|KID Mods}}</wrap> **KID Mods** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/kidtoolbarresource.png?nolink|Resource Production}}</wrap> **Resource Production** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildabandonedresource.png?nolink|Abandoned Resource}}</wrap> **Abandoned Resource** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildapnewmine.png?nolink|New Mine}}</wrap> **New Mine**

<wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/toolbarresource.png?nolink|Resource Production}}</wrap> **Resource Production** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/toolbarresourceproduce.png?nolink|Resource Production}}</wrap> **Resource Production** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildabandonedresource.png?nolink|Abandoned Resource}}</wrap> **Abandoned Resource** → <wrap toolbaricon>{{https://sprites.worldofbanished.com/buildapnewmine.png?nolink|New Mine}}</wrap> **New Mine**

The New Mine can also be reached by upgrading an existing [[buildings:mining:apoldmine|Old Mine]] or [[buildings:mining:apclosedmine|Closed Mine]].
