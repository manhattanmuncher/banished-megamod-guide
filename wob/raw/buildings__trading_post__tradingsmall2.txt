A Colonial Charter small industry supplier trading post. Must be placed on water. Merchants arrive by river boat offering raw construction and industry materials. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

| Resources Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, firewood, coal, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
