An Editor's Choice large trading post placed along a river. Traders work here and merchants arrive by river boat offering all resource categories. Supports up to 20 traders.

===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | Food, grain, vegetables, and fruit |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, health items, food, textiles, and clothing |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
