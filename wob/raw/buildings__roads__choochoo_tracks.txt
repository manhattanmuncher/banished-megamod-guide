The railway infrastructure from RedKetchup's Choo Choo mod — the decorative train tracks plus the railroad bridges and tunnels they run through. Lay tracks to build a rail line that ties together the Choo Choo trains, wagons and stations (which have their own pages). Tracks are built from wood and iron; bridges and tunnels cost more.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Track tiles** — Straight, Turn Left, Turn Right, Diagonal.
  * **Over-canal tracks** — Track Over Canal (4X / 6X / 8X spans, in New Bricks / Old Bricks finishes).
  * **Railroad bridges** — Wood Railroad Bridge, Wood Stone Railroad Bridge, Stone Railroad Bridge, Covered Railroad Bridge.
  * **Railroad tunnels** — Railroad Tunnel and Railroad Tunnel Deco (from the Choo Choo Deco Tunnel add-on).
