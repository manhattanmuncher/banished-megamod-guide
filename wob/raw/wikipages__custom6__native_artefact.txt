===== Overview =====




Native Artefact is a precious item valued for Its rarity or beauty in the Banished MegaMod. It is stored in a Barn and functions primarily as luxury trade goods, attracting favourable prices from merchants. At a trade value of 2500, it is highly prized by traveling merchants.
