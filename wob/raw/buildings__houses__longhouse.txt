===== Upgrades =====

The Native Longhouse upgrades to one of two paths:

^ Upgrade ^ Result ^
| Convert to a boarding house | [[buildings:houses:longhousehouse|Native Longhouse Storage]] becomes a boarding house for settlers |
| Convert to a storage building | [[buildings:storage:longhousestorage|Native Longhouse Storage]] becomes a 4,000-capacity storage building |
