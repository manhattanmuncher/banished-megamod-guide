An upgraded Medieval Creamery with improved efficiency.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Creamery (Upgrade A) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:dairy:newmedievalcreamery|Medieval Creamery]].
