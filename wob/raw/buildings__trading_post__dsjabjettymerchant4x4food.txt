A DS Jetty & Bridge food trading post. Traders work here and food merchants arrive by river boat offering a variety of food items. Must be placed on water.

See also: [[buildings:trading_post:dsjabjettymerchant4x4foodtex2|Jetty Food Merchant (Tex2)]] for the alternate texture variant.

===== Merchant =====

Multiple food merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food & Edibles Merchant | All food items (fruit, vegetables, grain, protein, and other edibles) |
| merchant of Brent's Veggies | Vegetables |
| Dry Grains Merchant | Grain |
| Henry Family Merchant | Fruit and vegetables |
| Fish, Meats & Nuts Trader | Protein (meat, fish, and nuts) |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
