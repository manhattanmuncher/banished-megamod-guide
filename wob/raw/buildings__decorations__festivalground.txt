Kid's Festival Ground — a set of fairground tents and decorations to build a festival site. The tents double as happiness sources, each covering a different need.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Food Tent** (5×5) — Goods happiness.
  * **Music Tent** (3×3) — Entertainment happiness.
  * **Exhibit Tent** (4×4) — Spirit happiness.
  * Assorted festival decorations.
