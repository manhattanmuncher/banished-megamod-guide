The Choo Choo: Train Engine is a Choo Choo mod building that functions as a trading post. A train driver spends Coins to purchase a wide variety of goods from food and resources to clothing and crafted items. Model 1 is cosmetically distinct from [[buildings:trading_post:chootrain2|Model 2]] but functionally identical.

===== Trades (Coins → Goods) =====

The train driver purchases goods using Coins. Coin costs vary by item value. A sample of available trades:

^ Coins  ^ Goods Purchased  ^
| 6   | Wood  |
| 10  | Iron  |
| 14  | Stone  |
| 22  | Blueberry, Mushroom, Onion, Root  |
| 24  | Coal  |
| 28  | Bean, Cabbage, Corn, Pepper, Potato, Pumpkin, Squash, Wheat, Barley  |
| 60  | Leather  |
| 65  | Apple, Cherry, Peach, Pear, Plum, Chestnut, Pecan, Walnut  |
| 80  | Beer, Ale  |
| 18  | Chicken, Beeswax  |
| 7   | Fish  |
| 12  | Egg  |
| 600 | Beef, Mutton, Venison  |

Many additional goods are available including clothing, tools, crafted items, and processed foods.
