The Trading Post is the vanilla Banished trading building. Traders facilitate trade with travelling merchants, and it provides a Goods happiness bonus to nearby citizens. MegaMod significantly expands the trade system: instead of vanilla's 5 general merchants, 15 specialized merchants visit covering every MegaMod storage category, and caravans arrive more frequently at every population tier.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Trading Post is found in the vanilla Banished **Social** building category.
