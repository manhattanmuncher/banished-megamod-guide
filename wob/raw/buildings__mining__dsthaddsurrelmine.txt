A massive DS Mods industrial mine — the Thadd Surrel & family operation — covering an 8 × 14 footprint and employing up to 55 Miners. Coal is the default product but the mine can be switched between Coal and Iron Ore. The pit imposes a Detraction happiness penalty in a 25-tile radius and depletes after 84,000 units of total production.
