An upgraded Medieval Archaeology site with improved efficiency, and the ability to process clay as well as stone.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Archaeology (Upgraded) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:crafting:newmedievalarcheology|Medieval Archaeology]].
