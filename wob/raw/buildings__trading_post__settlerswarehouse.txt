A Settlers warehouse and trading post where Workers receive merchant boats from five merchant types. Also functions as large local storage for a wide range of goods.

===== Merchants =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, and alcohol |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |
