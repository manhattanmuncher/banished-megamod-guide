A decorative stone garden-wall kit from RedKetchup's Garden Walls set, used to fence off gardens, line paths and frame buildings. The kit comes in two visual styles and a full range of wall sections, columns, gateways and matching props so walls can be run, turned and capped neatly. (The Garden Walls set's working buildings — well, greenhouse, florist, perfumery, stables, barns — have their own pages.)

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Wall sections** — Straight Short (1×1), Med (1×2), Long (1×3), plus Long sections with Openings and with Windows.
  * **Gateways** — Arch Door and Gate Door (1×3).
  * **Columns** — Straight, Corner, 3-Way, 4-Way, End, diagonal and soft-turn column pieces.
  * **Diagonals** — short diagonal straight runs.
  * **Props** — Lamp Post and Lamp Post 2, Pot of Tree 1–4, and a road-deco tile.
  * **Two styles** — original ("Fire") set and the "Old" set, each with the full piece range.
