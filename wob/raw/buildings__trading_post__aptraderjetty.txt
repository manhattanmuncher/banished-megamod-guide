===== Merchant =====

Four merchant types may visit. A Food merchant offers up to 15 food items (edible goods, fruit, vegetables, grain, and protein). A Goods merchant offers up to 5 items including wood, stone, iron, firewood, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies. A General merchant offers up to 20 items including tools, herbs, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, and seeds. A Seed merchant offers up to 4 crop or orchard seeds. All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
