An NMT Docks trading post. Traders receive merchants arriving by river boat; provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present. Can be upgraded to the [[buildings:trading_post:nmttradingpoststonedock|Trading Post (NMT Stone Dock)]].

===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, coal, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, coal, tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, and alcohol |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |
