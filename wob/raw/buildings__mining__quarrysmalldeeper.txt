The Deeper Small Quarry is the final tier of the Small Quarry upgrade chain, digging to unlimited depths for Stone and Salt. It has no production cap.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:quarrysmalldeep|Deep Small Quarry]].
