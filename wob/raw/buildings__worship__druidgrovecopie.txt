A Druidic grove staffed by a Priest, providing Spirit happiness to nearby residents within a radius of 50 tiles and accommodating up to 50 attendees. This building uses the same in-game name and stats as the [[buildings:worship:druidgrove|Druid Grove]] but features a single mesh variant.
