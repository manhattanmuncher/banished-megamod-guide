====== Starting Conditions ======
----

Starting conditions are chosen when starting a new game. They determine how many families you begin with, what resources and tools are provided, which structures (if any) are pre-built, and which crops, orchard trees, and livestock are seeded on the map. Starting conditions do not affect terrain shape or climate.

**Adam and Eve** (AE) conditions start with a single couple — one family, no pre-generated children.

----

===== Base Difficulty Tiers =====

Most starting conditions — vanilla and mod alike — use one of these four resource templates. Mod series typically differ in their pre-built structures and the specific crops/trees/animals available on the map, not in the resource amounts.

^ Difficulty ^ Families ^ Food ^ Firewood ^ Clothing ^ Tools ^ Wood ^ Stone ^ Iron ^ Crop Seeds ^ Orchard Seeds ^ Livestock ^ Pre-built Structures ^
| Hard | 4 | 1,200 | 100 | 20 | 20 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | Storage Cart |
| Medium | 5 | 1,800 | 200 | 30 | 40 | 120 | 60 | 40 | 2 | 1 | 0 | Storage Barn + Storage Yard |
| Easy | 6 | 2,400 | 400 | 40 | 50 | 150 | 70 | 50 | 2 | 2 | 1 | Storage Barn + Storage Yard + 6 Wood Houses |
| Adam & Eve | 1 | 250 | 100 | 7 | 7 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | Storage Cart |

----

===== Vanilla Starting Conditions =====

The three vanilla starting conditions (Hard, Medium, Easy) follow the base templates exactly.
