===== Overview =====

Roasted Chestnut is a food item made by roasting Chestnuts over a fire. Citizens eat it as part of their diet, contributing to variety and overall happiness. Its trade value is low at 2. It is made at the [[buildings:food:marketfallfood|Market Fall Food]] and the [[buildings:food:xmasmarket|Xmas Market]]. (It was previously two separate items, the Market version and the Xmas version; they are now one.)
