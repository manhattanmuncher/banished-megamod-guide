A bright-themed Gothic Fantasy market. Distributes a wide variety of goods to nearby settlers and provides Goods happiness when stocked. Same stats as the Gothic Fantasy 1 variant.

===== Stocks =====

  * Edible food (all types)
  * Fuel and Coal and Coke
  * Tools
  * Health items
  * Clothing
  * Iron and Logs
  * Textiles
  * Crafted items (Custom 0)
  * Forged items (Custom 1)
  * Fabrics (Custom 2)
