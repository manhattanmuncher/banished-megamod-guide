A Forest Trader small trading post. Traders work here and merchants arrive by river boat offering food, goods, and general wares. All merchants accept only textiles (furs), wood, and firewood as payment. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Three merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, minerals, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, industrial fuel, raw materials, building supplies, precious goods, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, minerals, and alcohol; also crop and orchard seeds |

All merchants accept only textiles, wood, and firewood as payment.
