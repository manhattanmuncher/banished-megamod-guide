Ghost versions of the Colonial Charter decorative ponds that provide Safety happiness and fire suppression within a 20-tile radius. As ghost buildings, they have a 0×0 footprint and can be placed literally anywhere on the map. They cannot be removed with the standard removal tool — use the building's own interface to remove them. Two variants are available; both share the same appearance as the [[buildings:wells:ccpond|Pond (Colonial Charter)]] variants.
