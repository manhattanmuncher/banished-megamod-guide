Mexican Fantasy ghosted hitching posts that attract nomad immigrants. Each variant limits the maximum size of arriving nomad groups as a percentage of total population. Free to place with no footprint restriction. Seven variants are available, including a no-nomads version to suppress nomad attraction; the 0-variant is limited to one instance.
