A New Medieval Town library staffed by a Priest, providing Spirit happiness to nearby residents within a radius of 45 tiles and accommodating up to 90 attendees.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Library (Upgrade A) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:worship:newmedievallibrary|Medieval Library]].
