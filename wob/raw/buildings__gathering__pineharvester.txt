A Maritimes Pine Set harvester's cache. Gatherers harvest wild pine resources within a 25-tile radius, collecting Pine Resin, Pine Bough, Bough, and Flax. Must be used alongside the Pine Forestry Tower, which plants the pine trees that spawn these resources.
