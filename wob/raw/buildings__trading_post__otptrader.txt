An Old Town Ports trading post. Merchants arrive by river boat offering food, goods, seeds, and livestock. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, raw materials, building supplies, precious goods, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Food, wood, stone, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, tools, health items, textiles, clothing, alcohol, and crop and orchard seeds |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
