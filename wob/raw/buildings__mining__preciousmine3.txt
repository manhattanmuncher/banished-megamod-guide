The Precious Mine (Upgrade 3) is the final tier of the Colonial Charter Precious Mine. It shares the same production stats as earlier tiers, producing Silver, Gold, and Gems randomly. This upgrade uses Lamp Oil to light the deepest mine shafts.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade only — not available in the toolbar. Build by upgrading a [[buildings:mining:preciousmine2|Precious Mine (Upgrade 2)]].
