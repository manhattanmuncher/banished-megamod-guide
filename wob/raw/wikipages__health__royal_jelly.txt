===== Overview =====




Royal Jelly is a health item gathered or crafted in the Banished MegaMod. It is delivered to the Herbalist, who uses it to treat sick citizens and maintain community wellbeing. It carries a trade value of 8, making it a worthwhile commodity for trade.
