===== Overview =====




Reeds are a miscellaneous trade good available in the Banished MegaMod. They are stored in a Barn and mainly useful for trading with passing merchant caravans. Their trade value is low at 1, but they play an important role in the colony's production chains.

----

===== Reeds Mods =====

==== Procuring / where to get ====
  * Produced at the [[buildings:gathering:dockreedc|Reed Farm]]
  * Produced at the [[buildings:sand_clay:shorepit|Shore House]]
  * Grows wild near water and swamps; can be hand-harvested with a Scythe

==== Processing / what to do with ====

  * Used at the [[buildings:clothing:tailor|Tailor]] (into Reed Coats)
  * Used at the [[buildings:crafting:dockshopcraft|Dock Workshop]] (into Reed Coats)

  * Used at the [[buildings:fuel:firebundler|Bundling Shed]] (into Firebundles)
  * Used at the [[buildings:preparing:swampreedpacker|Swamp Reed Packer]] (into Fuel Bricks)
