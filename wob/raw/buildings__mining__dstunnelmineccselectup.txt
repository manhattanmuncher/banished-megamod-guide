Upgrade of the Tunnel Mine Select for significantly more resource production (40,000 limit) and up to 13 miners. The player continues to select which material to produce.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:dstunnelmineccselect|Tunnel Mine Select]].

----
