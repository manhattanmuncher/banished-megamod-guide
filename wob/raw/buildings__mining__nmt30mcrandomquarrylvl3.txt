The Random Quarry Lvl3 is an upgraded Random Quarry with further increased production capacity. It can be upgraded to continue expanding output.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:nmt30mcrandomquarrylvl2|Random Quarry Lvl2]].

----

===== Produces =====

  * [[WikiPages:stone:stone|Stone]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:iron_ore|Iron Ore]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:copper_ore|Copper Ore]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:salt|Salt]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:coal|Coal]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:gold_ore|Gold Ore]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:silver_ore|Silver Ore]]
  * [[WikiPages:coalfuel:gemstone_rough|Gemstone (Rough)]]

One resource is produced randomly per work cycle.
