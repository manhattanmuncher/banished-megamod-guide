====== Professions ======
----

Professions are the job roles assigned to adult citizens. Each building employs one or more professions. Citizens assigned to a job are more productive than laborers, and educated citizens are more productive still. Two professions — Laborer and Builder — are transient: citizens automatically shift into and out of these roles as needed, and cannot be directly assigned.

Each profession carries a background rate of accidental on-the-job deaths, independent of disease or old age. The **Deaths / 100 / yr** column shows expected deaths per 100 workers per year.
