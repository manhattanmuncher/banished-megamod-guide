EB Small Village (embx61) wooden bridges. Drag to place across water; cost scales per tile. Available as open and roofed (covered) versions, in standard and "Warm" (winter) finishes, with fixed-length 1/2/4/8-tile span pieces.

----

===== Variants =====

^ Variant ^
| Wooden Bridge (open) |
| Wooden Bridge, Roofed |
| Wooden Bridge, Warm |
| Wooden Bridge, Roofed Warm |

Each is available as a draggable span plus fixed 1/2/4/8-tile pieces. All share the same per-tile cost (3 work, 5 Wood).
