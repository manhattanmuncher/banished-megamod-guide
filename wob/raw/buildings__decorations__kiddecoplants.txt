A large set of decorative exotic plants from Kid's Deco Plants mod, for greening gardens, courtyards and greenhouses. All are purely cosmetic and free to place.

----

===== Variants =====

  * Bromeliads, Desert Rose, Dragon Arum, Elephant Ear, and many more exotic species (each often in several size/colour options).
