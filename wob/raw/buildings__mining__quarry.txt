The Quarry is the vanilla Banished quarry. The MegaMod reduces max production to 1,100 (from vanilla's 2,000) and adds upgrade paths when depleted: it can be upgraded into the Deep Quarry chain for increased Stone output, or the Quarry (Renew) chain for extended production.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Quarry is found in the vanilla Banished **Mines** building category.
