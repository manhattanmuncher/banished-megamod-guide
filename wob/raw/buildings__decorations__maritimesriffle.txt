A set of Necora Maritimes terrain-painting and water-feature tools. Use the **Riffle** pieces to add streams and waterfalls to rivers, and the **Painting** tools to flatten, dig, slope, and texture riverbeds and banks (driftwood, pebbles, edges) at several depths (0 / 0.25 / 0.50 / 0.75 m).

----

===== Variants =====

^ Group ^ Tools ^
| Riffle water features | Riffle Stream pieces (Riffle 01–06) |
| Painting (depths 0–0.75m) | Center, Corner, Diagonal, Edge, Driftwood, Pebbles |
| Terraforming | Flatten/Dig (0 to −0.25m+), Low, Slope, Waterfall |
