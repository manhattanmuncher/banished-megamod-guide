A Colonial Charter native longhouse converted into a boarding house; temporarily houses up to 6 settlers across 5 apartments.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from a pre-placed Native Longhouse on certain Colonial Charter Frontier map types; obtained by bartering peacefully with the natives.

----
