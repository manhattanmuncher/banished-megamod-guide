A New Medieval Town barbershop staffed by a Doctor, providing Health happiness to nearby residents within a radius of 30 tiles and treating up to 90 patients.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval BarberShop (Upgrade E) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:hospital:newmedievalbarbershop|Medieval BarberShop]].
