A Colonial Charter Frontier native wigwam converted to a storage building with a capacity of 1,000 units. Obtained by bartering peacefully with the natives.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from a pre-placed [[buildings:houses:wigwamhouse|Native WigWam House]] on certain Colonial Charter Frontier map types; obtained by bartering peacefully with the natives.
