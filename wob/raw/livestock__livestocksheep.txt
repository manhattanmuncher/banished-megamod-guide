Sheep are vanilla livestock raised at a Pasture. They are shorn for Wool periodically and yield Mutton when slaughtered. The MegaMod adds Fertilizer to the slaughter products and slightly increases wool production frequency compared to vanilla.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Raised at a Pasture.
