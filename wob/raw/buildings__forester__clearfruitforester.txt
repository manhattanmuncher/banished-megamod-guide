====== Clear Fruit (Grow Hunt Fish) ======
----

<WRAP right></WRAP>

A Grow Hunt Fish removal zone tool. Draw an area to have laborers remove fruit tree natural resources (Apple, Cherry, Peach, Pear, and Plum trees) from the map. No build cost and no workers assigned — the zone is cleared by the general labor pool.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Found on the vanilla Demolish/Removal toolbar.
