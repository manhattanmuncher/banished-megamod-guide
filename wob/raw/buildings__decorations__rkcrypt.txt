A decorative stone crypt from the RK Editor's Choice Edition. Despite sitting on the Creepy Cemetery toolbar it is a standalone decoration rather than a working cemetery — it radiates **Spirit** happiness to nearby homes within a radius of 30, like a small shrine or memorial.
