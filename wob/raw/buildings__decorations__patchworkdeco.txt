Kid - Patchwork decorative crop plots — purely cosmetic versions of the mod's 18 growing patches, for dressing up farms and gardens without an actual worker or yield. Free to place, with a remove tool.

----

===== Variants =====

  * Barley, Bean, Brussel Sprouts, Cabbage, Carrots, Corn
  * Mushroom, Oat, Onion, Pepper, Potato, Pumpkin
  * Squash, Strawberry, Sunflower, Sweet Potato, Tomato, Wheat
