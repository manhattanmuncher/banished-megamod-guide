Tea Seeds are used to plant a Tea Leaves tree in an [[buildings:orchards:start|Orchard]]. Farmers harvest [[WikiPages:health:tea_leaves|Tea Leaves]] using an Axe. Fruit cycles take 4 months; trees reach maturity in 16 months.

----

===== Procuring =====

Can be purchased from merchants at any [[buildings:trading_post:start|Trading Post]].
