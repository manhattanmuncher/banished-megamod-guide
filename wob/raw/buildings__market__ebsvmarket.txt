===== Stocks =====

  * Edible food (all types)
  * Fuel (firewood)
  * Tools
  * Health items
  * Clothing
  * Iron and Logs
  * Textiles
  * Crafted items (Custom 0)
  * Fabrics (Custom 2)
  * Industrial fuel (Custom 3)
  * Precious items (Custom 6)
