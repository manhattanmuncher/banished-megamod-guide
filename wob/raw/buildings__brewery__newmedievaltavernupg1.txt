An upgraded Medieval Tavern with improved brewing efficiency.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Tavern (Upgrade 1) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:brewery:newmedievaltavern|Medieval Tavern]].
