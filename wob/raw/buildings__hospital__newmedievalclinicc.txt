A New Medieval Town clinic staffed by a Doctor, treating up to 15 patients at a time.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval Clinic (Upgrade C) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:hospital:newmedievalclinic|Medieval Clinic]].
