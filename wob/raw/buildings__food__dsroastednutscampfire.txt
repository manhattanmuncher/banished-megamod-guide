A DS Mods open-air campfire where a Chef roasts a wide variety of nuts (Almonds, Chestnuts, Hazelnuts, Pecans, Pistachios, Walnuts, Acorns) into Roasted Nuts using a small amount of Firewood. The campfire accepts either a single nut type or any pairing of two nut types per recipe.

===== Recipes =====

All recipes consume **1 [[WikiPages:fuel:firewood|Firewood]]** in addition to the nut(s) and produce [[WikiPages:food:roasted_nuts|Roasted Nuts]].

==== Two-nut mix recipes ====

Each pairing uses **34 of each nut + 1 Firewood**. Available pairings:

  * Almond + Chestnut, Hazelnut, Pecan, Pistachio, Walnut, Acorns
  * Chestnut + Hazelnut, Pecan, Pistachio, Walnut, Acorns
  * Hazelnut + Pecan, Pistachio, Walnut, Acorns
  * Pecan + Pistachio, Walnut, Acorns
  * Pistachio + Walnut, Acorns
  * Walnut + Acorns
