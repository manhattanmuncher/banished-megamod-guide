===== Overview =====




Royal Armor is a forged item crafted from metal in the Banished MegaMod. It is produced at a forge or smithy and stored in a Barn, serving purposes in trade, construction, or equipping citizens. At a trade value of 65, it is highly prized by traveling merchants.
