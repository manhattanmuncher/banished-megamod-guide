A New Medieval 30-tile canal set trading post (Old Church texture). Merchants arrive by river boat offering all resource categories. Must be placed on water. Provides Goods happiness to settlers within 20 tiles when a trader is present.

===== Merchant =====

Five merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Food Merchant | All food items |
| Goods Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, tools, textiles, clothing, alcohol, crafted goods, forged goods, fabric, raw materials, building supplies, precious goods, and miscellaneous items |
| General Merchant | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, industrial fuel, tools, health items, food, textiles, clothing, and alcohol |
| Seed Merchant | Crop and orchard seeds |
| Livestock Merchant | Livestock |
