A glowing tower from Kid's Ice Castle winter set. **The Light** signals to wandering nomads, attracting a group (about 12% of the current population) to seek refuge roughly once a year — the nomad-attracting counterpart to the [[buildings:town_halls:icecastle|Ice Castle]] town hall, which does not draw nomads.
