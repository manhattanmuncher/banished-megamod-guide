A large set of decorative farmyard, garden, playground and street pieces from **Kid's Workshop** mod. Almost everything is purely cosmetic and free to place; the **Happy Square** pieces additionally emit a small happiness boost within a 16-tile radius. Pieces are grouped into several pop-out toolbars. Two pieces in the Farmyard group — [[buildings:fields:kwfarmyard01|Farmyard 01]] and [[buildings:fields:kwfarmyard02|Farmyard 02]] — are actual food-producing buildings (not decorative) and have their own dedicated pages; see those for stats and recipes.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Deco Objects** — Rocking Chair, Flower Pot, Flower, Doorstep
  * **Bush** — decorative bush
  * **Paths** — Block Path, Gravel Path
  * **5×5 Cover** and **Awning Covers** — six awning variants (01A–03A, 01B–03B) plus a 5×5 ground cover
  * **Playground** — Deco People, Bench, Sandbox, Slide, Swing
  * **Farmyard** — Dung Pile, Hay Pile, Groundcover (decorative); [[buildings:fields:kwfarmyard01|Farmyard 01]] and [[buildings:fields:kwfarmyard02|Farmyard 02]] (food-producing, see their own pages)
  * **Happy Squares** (happiness, 16-tile radius) — Entertainment, Goods, Health, Safety, Spirit
