====== Climate ======
----

Climate determines the temperature range, seasonal weather patterns, and tornado frequency for a map. It is chosen when starting a new game and cannot be changed afterward. Temperature controls when crops can grow, how much firewood citizens consume, and how often citizens get sick.

Banished tracks twelve weather periods per year (early spring through late winter). Citizens need clothing and firewood to survive cold periods; they only need clothing in warm climates.

Growing crops require temperatures above freezing (32 °F). The winter low and summer high below are calculated directly from each climate's base temperature and seasonal range (base − range / base + range). Climates where the winter low stays above 32 °F allow year-round farming; those where the summer high barely exceeds 32 °F offer little or no growing season.

Tornadoes spawn based on years and population. The **Tornado risk** column shows the average years between events at low population; large towns attract tornadoes more frequently.

===== Technical Parameters =====

The values below come directly from each climate's RSC definition file.

**Base Temp** — year-round average temperature in °F.\\
**± Range** — seasonal swing; summer high = base + range, winter low = base − range.\\
**Daily Swing** — how much temperature varies within a single weather period beyond the seasonal curve; higher values mean unpredictable warm and cold snaps within a season.\\
**Yr/Yr Temp** — how much the base temperature shifts year to year; higher values mean some years run significantly warmer or colder than normal.\\
**Yr/Yr Weather** — how much precipitation patterns shift year to year; higher values mean some years are far wetter or drier than the seasonal averages suggest.

Siberian's daily swing of −20 is unusual — a negative tolerance value means temperatures within each period tend to run //colder// than the seasonal curve, making winters even more brutal than the −50 °F baseline suggests.

Warm Arid and Harsh Arid include a population component of 10,000 in their tornado formula, making tornadoes effectively impossible at normal town sizes despite the nominal 600-year period.

===== Notes =====

  * **[CC]** = Colonial Charter climate. **DS** = Discrepancy climate. Unmarked climates are vanilla Banished or bundled mod climates.
  * **Mild / Fair / Harsh** share the same base temperature (50 °F, ±30 °F swing). The difference is how much weather varies year to year: Mild is the most predictable, Harsh the most erratic.
  * **Very Mild** also shares the same temperature profile as Fair, but with virtually no tornado risk and the most stable weather of any climate.
  * **More Snow** is the only climate with a peak summer temperature below freezing — crops cannot grow at all.
  * **Perfect** has no seasonal swing (range = 0), meaning no snow, no cold, no variation — a scenario rather than a realistic climate.
  * **Siberian** has the widest temperature range (±75 °F), making winters lethal and summers hot, but the growing window is brief.
