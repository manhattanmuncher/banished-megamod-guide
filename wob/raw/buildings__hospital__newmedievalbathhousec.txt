A New Medieval Town bathhouse staffed by a Worker, providing Health happiness to nearby residents within a radius of 60 tiles and accommodating up to 2000 attendees.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Medieval BathHouse (Upgrade C) is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:hospital:newmedievalbathhouse|Medieval BathHouse]].
