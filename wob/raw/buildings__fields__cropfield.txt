The Crop Field is the vanilla Banished farming zone. Farmers plant and harvest crops within the designated area. Designation is free; no build cost.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Crop Field is found in the vanilla Banished **Food Production** building category.

----

===== Produces =====

Output depends on the selected crop type.
