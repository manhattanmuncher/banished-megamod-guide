A set of DS Jetty & Bridge walkable jetty deck pieces, used to build piers and platforms over water. Combine the 1×1, 2×1, and 3×1 sections, corners, high/low decks, angles, and stairs. Every piece comes in two texture styles (T1 light-brown and T2 blue-grey).

----

===== Variants =====

^ Piece ^ Size ^
| Jetty section (1×1 / 2×1 / 3×1) | 1×1 – 3×1 |
| Jetty corner | 1×1 |
| High / Low jetty | 1×1 |
| Jetty angle | 1×1 / 2×2 |
| Jetty stairs (low / high) | 1×1 |
| Jetty pole / fence rails | deco |

Each piece is available in Texture #1 and Texture #2 styles.
