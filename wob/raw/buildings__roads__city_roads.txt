A large set of RedKetchup pre-formed paved road blocks for laying down city streets and plazas in one click instead of tile by tile. Roads come in three paving textures and a wide range of block shapes and widths — straights, ends, soft turns, sharp turns, diagonals, 3-way and 4-way intersections — built from stone scaled to the block size.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Three paving textures** — City Roads Texture 01, 02 and 03 (alternate cobble/paving looks).
  * **Widths** — 1-tile (Xtra), 3-tile (3X) and 4-tile (4X) wide blocks, plus a "2T" two-texture series.
  * **Shapes** (per width) — straight blocks (3×1 … 4×4), Ends, Turns, Soft Turns (left/right), Diagonals, T-junctions (3-Way / 4-Way), plus 1×1 sidewalk and road-patch fillers.
