A local market in the Bryggen TownHouses style. Stocks food, clothing, tools, health items, and firewood for nearby settlers. Provides Goods happiness when a worker is assigned.

  * Food (Grain, Fruit, Vegetables, Meat/Fish)
  * Tools and Health items
  * Clothing and Firewood
