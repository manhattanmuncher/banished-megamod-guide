A massive 12 × 12 mine with default crew of 10 Miners. Produces refined Iron directly from the seam. The pit imposes a Detraction happiness penalty in a 30-tile radius and is exhausted after a single unit of production — see this as a curiosity / test building rather than a regular industrial mine.
