The Mine (Renew 6) is the sixth renewal upgrade in the Editor's Choice Iron Mine chain. It increases the production cap to 14,000 and can be upgraded to the final tier.

----

===== Where to Find =====

Upgrade from the [[buildings:mining:ironminerenew5|Mine (Renew 5)]].
