The water-channel building blocks of RedKetchup's NMT 3.1 Canal Set — the straight runs, turns, corners, junctions and crossing bridges used to route a canal across the map. They carry no storage or workers on their own; the canal's working pieces (docks, mills and trading posts) attach to these channels and are documented on their own ''nmt30canal…'' pages. Channels come in eight masonry styles and three widths so a canal can be themed and sized to fit.

----

===== Variants =====

  * **Widths** — 4× (narrow), 6× (medium) and 8× (wide) channel spans.
  * **Shapes** — Straight runs, Corners (left/right), Turns, 3-Way junctions, 4-Way junctions, and Bridges that carry land traffic across the canal.
  * **Eight styles** (the "C1–C8" suffixes) — C1 Classic Stone, C2 Old Bricks, C3 Red Bricks, C4 Sandstones, C5 Fire Bricks, C6 Old Church, C7 Dark Stones, C8 Old Wood. ''R'' pieces are the mirrored (reversed) version of a shape.
