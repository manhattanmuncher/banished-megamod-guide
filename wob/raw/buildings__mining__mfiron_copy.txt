===== Overview =====

The Iron Deposit (Copy) is a variant of the [[buildings:mining:mfiron|Iron Deposit]] from the Mexican Fantasy mod with a doubled maximum production of 20. This building has no toolbar registration and cannot be placed from the build menu in the current mod version.
