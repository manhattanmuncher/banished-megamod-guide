The Old Mine is an Abandoned Places building that produces Stone and occasional Gold. It can be upgraded to a New Mine for more material options.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Old Mine is not built directly from the toolbar. It is created by upgrading an existing [[buildings:mining:apclosedmine|Closed Mine]].
