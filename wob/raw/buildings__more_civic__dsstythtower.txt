Styth Tower is the original DS Mods watchtower — a smaller Safety-radius alternative to the [[buildings:more_civic:dsnewstythtower|New Styth Tower]]. Village Guards confiscate a varied supply of goods from bandits in the surrounding area and deliver them to the town's stockpiles. Only one Styth Tower can be built per map.
