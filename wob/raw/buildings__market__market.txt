The Market is the vanilla Banished market building. Vendors distribute goods to citizens, providing a Goods happiness bonus within a large 45-tile radius. MegaMod increases its storage capacity and lets it stock every storage category (vanilla is limited to a smaller set), with fill ratios rebalanced across the expanded goods list.

----

===== Where to Find =====

The Market is found in the vanilla Banished **Social** building category.
