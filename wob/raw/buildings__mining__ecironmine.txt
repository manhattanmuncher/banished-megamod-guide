The RK Editor's Choice mine — a massive 12 × 12 pit employing up to 30 Miners. Iron Ore is the default product but the mine can be switched between Iron Ore and Coal. The pit imposes a Detraction happiness penalty in a 30-tile radius and depletes after 8,000 units of total production.
