A Kid - Nomads decorative sign that attracts nomad immigrants. Each variant limits the maximum size of arriving nomad groups as a percentage of total population. Free to place with no footprint restriction, max 1 instance per variant. Seven variants are available — the plain "Nomad Sign" variant is purely decorative with no immigration effect (its immigration behavior is not active in the underlying data).
