A DS Jetty & Bridge large general trading post. Traders work here and merchants arrive by river boat offering a wide variety of goods across all categories. Must be placed on water.

See also: [[buildings:trading_post:dsjabjettymerchant8x4generaltex2|General Jetty Merchant (Tex2)]] for the alternate texture variant.

===== Merchant =====

Multiple merchant types may visit by river boat:

^ Merchant ^ Offers ^
| Smithon General Trader | Food, wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, herbs, textiles, and clothing |
| Banished Import Co. Merchant | Food, wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, herbs, textiles, clothing, crafted goods, and forged goods |
| Resource Trader | Wood, stone, iron, fuel, tools, herbs, textiles, industrial fuel, raw materials, and building supplies |
| Merchant from Thelma's Seeds & Foods | Food items (fruit, vegetables, grain, and protein) |

All merchants accept any settlement goods as payment.
