P&H Brewery (Right Extension) is a right-hand-side extension building that adds brewing capacity to the [[buildings:brewery:dspickandhen|Pick & Hen Tavern]]. A single Brewer produces the same range of Ales, Fruit Wines, Mead, and Mulled drinks as the main tavern. Up to two of these extensions can be built per town.
