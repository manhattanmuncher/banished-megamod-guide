===== Overview =====




Silver Bracelets are a precious item valued for their rarity or beauty in the Banished MegaMod. They are stored in a Barn and function primarily as luxury trade goods, attracting favourable prices from merchants. At a trade value of 600, they are highly prized by traveling merchants.
