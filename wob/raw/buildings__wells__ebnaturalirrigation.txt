The EB (embx61) Natural Irrigation system — a modular water-distribution network for transporting water across the colony. Build a **Water Lift** or **Water Well Lift** to raise water, then run **channel, gutter, corner, curve, culvert, bridge, and gate** pieces to carry it where needed, ending at a [[buildings:wells:ebirrigationendwelln|Water Reservoir]].

----

===== Key Pieces =====

^ Piece ^ Size ^ Build Cost ^
| Channel 1x Straight | 3 × 1 | 10 work, 1 [[WikiPages:wood:log|Wood]] |
| Water Lift (pump) | 4 × 3 | 32 work, 28 Wood, 4 Iron — Worker raises river water |
| Water Well Lift | 3 × 3 | 24 work, 18 Wood — Worker raises ground water |
| Corner / Curve / Gutter / Culvert / Bridge / Gate pieces | varies | low Wood cost each |

Includes both standard and ghosted (no-footprint) network pieces.
