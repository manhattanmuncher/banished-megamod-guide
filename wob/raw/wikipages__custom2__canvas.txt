===== Overview =====




Canvas is a processed fabric or refined textile in the Banished MegaMod. It is produced from raw fibers and stored in a Barn, used as an ingredient in clothing and other crafted goods. It carries a trade value of 12, making it a worthwhile commodity for trade.
