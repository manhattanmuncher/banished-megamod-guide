The original RK Editor Choice version of the Garden Walls Greenhouse. A Botanist uses Water and Fertilizer to grow a wide variety of crops year-round. Supports 32 different crop types including grains and Seedlings, making it more versatile than the newer Garden Walls Greenhouse.
