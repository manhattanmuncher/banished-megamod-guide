Starting a new game

The new-game screen has three choices that can't be changed later. The in-game descriptions are short, so here is what each one actually decides, from the wiki's Starting Conditions, Terrain and Climate pages.

ChoiceDecidesDoes not affect
Starting conditionHow many families, starting food, firewood, clothing, tools, building materials, pre-built buildings, and which seeds, trees and animals are on the mapMap shape, climate
Terrain (map type)Map shape: flat or hilly, how many rivers and lakes, ground lookClimate, crop growth, tornadoes
ClimateTemperature, growing season, firewood use, how often tornadoes comeMap shape

2. Terrain (map type)

Terrain only sets the shape of the map. Most of MegaMod's many terrains are versions of four base shapes:

ShapeLooks likeExamples
ValleysGentle slopes, rivers, some lakes. The default.Valleys, Valleys More Rivers, [CC] Cold Valleys, Open Green Valley
PlainsOpen, flat land: room for big farms.Prairie, Flat, [CC] Flat Plains, [CC] Verdant Plains, Plains with/without Lake
MountainsRugged, fewer flat building areas.Mountains, Harsh Mountains, [CC] Bleak Mountains, Mountain Lakes
LakesLots of water.Big Lake, Many Lakes, Lakes, [CC] Lake Waters, [CC] Water World, Swamp
  • The RK Editor Choice terrains (Pine Wood, Misty, Equinox, Enchanted and so on) are reskins: each comes as Valley, Plain, Mountains and Lakes with a different look.
  • Watch out for deserts: Red Desert has no rivers and no lakes. A Trading Post must be built on a river or lake, because merchants arrive by boat (Trading). No water also means no fishing.
  • Building on water? Pick a water-heavy map such as [CC] Water World, Big Lake or Many Lakes (Life on the Water).

A safe first setup

  1. Starting condition: Medium (or Easy to learn).
  2. Terrain: Valleys: flat enough to build, and it has rivers for a Trading Post.
  3. Climate: [CC] Very Mild (no tornadoes) or Fair.

Then follow First 10 steps.
