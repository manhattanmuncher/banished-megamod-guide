Unhappy, idle people

Unhappy citizens idle a lot. Markets, Wells, Taverns, Trading Posts, Cemeteries and Chapels raise happiness; Mines and Quarries lower it for people living nearby. Births and marriages help; deaths, sickness, starvation and freezing hurt.

Disease

  • Prevent it: a varied diet (grain, fruit, vegetables, protein) and herbs from a [[b:buildings:gathering:herbalist]].
  • When it hits: a [[b:buildings:hospital:doctorhouse]] slows the spread and lowers deaths. Doctors can't catch disease.
  • Accepting nomads grows the town fast but can raise the risk of an outbreak.

Fire

  • Fire spreads to neighbours about once a day and needs 100 units of water to put out. Up to 50 citizens fight it.
  • Stone buildings ignite less and spread fire less (33%) than wood (50%).
  • Dock buildings (80% spread) and the DS Lumber Mill (ignites every 5 years, 80% spread) are the biggest risks: keep them away from dense housing.

Tornadoes

No warning and no defence. The only choice is the climate at game start: [CC] Very Mild never has them; Marine and DS Temperate almost never do.
