Nomads

Nomads are groups of new citizens who arrive and ask to join your town. They are the fastest way to grow, and MegaMod adds buildings that control how big the groups are.

How it works

  1. Unlock it Once your town has a Town Hall, a Trading Post and a Market, groups of nomads occasionally arrive and ask to join (Citizens).
  2. Accept or refuse Accepting grows the population fast, but may raise the risk of disease outbreaks.
  3. Be ready before you accept New people need homes, food, firewood, clothing and tools. Check you have spare houses and stock first.
  4. Cover the disease risk A [[b:buildings:gathering:herbalist]] and a [[b:buildings:hospital:doctorhouse]] near the houses (Death and cemeteries).
Not every town hall allows nomads: the wiki lists the [[b:buildings:town_halls:icecastle]] as “no nomads”.

Nomad attractors

MegaMod adds buildings that attract nomads and set the maximum group size as a percentage of your population. Toolbar: Town Services → Nomad.

Pick your group size (free, no footprint)

Each of these comes in variants: 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%, 50% and 100% of your population. Small percentages mean small, manageable groups; 100% can double your town at once.

  • [[b:buildings:nomads:nomadsigna]], [[b:buildings:nomads:nomadlampa]], [[b:buildings:nomads:nomadwella]] (Kid - Nomads): max 1 of each variant. The plain variant without a number is decoration only.
  • [[b:buildings:nomads:fpscarecrownomads]] (Friendly Pumpkin), [[b:buildings:nomads:whtetheringpost]] (Westward Ho), [[b:buildings:nomads:fairygardenmagicportal]] (Fairy Garden).
  • [[b:buildings:nomads:mfhitchingpost]] (Mexican Fantasy) also has a No Nomads variant that suppresses nomads entirely.

A simple plan

  1. Build the Town Hall, Trading Post and Market.
  2. Place one small attractor, for example a 5% or 10% sign, so groups stay small.
  3. Keep 1–2 empty houses and a food buffer ready.
  4. Want no nomads for a while? Use the Hitching Post's No Nomads variant.

The wiki also lists hostels and boarding houses that house settlers “temporarily”, but it doesn't link them to nomads. See Boarding Houses.
