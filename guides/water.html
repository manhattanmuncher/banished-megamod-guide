Life on the Water

The wiki has no step-by-step tutorial for a town on water, but its building pages say which buildings must or can be placed on water. This guide puts them together, so you can build a whole town on the water.

Where to find them: the Life on the Water toolbar holds 514 buildings. The biggest sets are NMT 3.1 Canal Set, DS Jetty & Bridge, NMT Docks (wood) and NMT Stone Docks, Docks (Colonial Charter), Maritimes Dock and Quay Wall. Search any building name to see its exact toolbar path.

How placement works

Each building page says how it is placed:

  • “Must be placed on water”: water only, for example fishing docks and dock homes.
  • “Buildable on water or on land”: either, for example the DS Jetty houses.
  • “For placement on a small / large / high platform”: needs a platform first, for example the Fish n Ships cabins.
  • Bridges: drag over water from shore to shore; the cost grows with every tile.

Step by step

  1. Lay walkways and platforms [[b:buildings:decorations:dsjabjettyplatforms]] are walkable deck pieces for piers and platforms over water: 1×1 to 3×1 sections, corners, high and low decks, angles and stairs. Alternatives: [[b:buildings:decorations:maritimesdockset]] (walkable piers) and [[b:buildings:bridges:dsstonebridgewalkways]] (stone bridges and raised causeways; a 2×3 section costs 13 Stone + 8 Lumber + 2 Wood + 1 Iron).
  2. Protect it from fire first Dock buildings are the biggest fire risk in the game: they catch fire about every 11 years at population 100, and fire spreads to neighbours 80% of the time (wood houses: 20 years, 50%) (Disasters). [[b:buildings:wells:wooddock]] are dock platform pieces that give fire suppression and Safety happiness within 20 tiles, for 2, 4 or 6 Logs. Spread them through the town. Stone versions exist for many NMT dock buildings, and stone spreads fire less (33%).
  3. Homes on water — see the table below.
  4. Storage [[b:buildings:storage:dockstorage]] (50 Logs) and [[b:buildings:storage:dockstoragesmall]] (40 Logs) are storage barns built over water as part of a dock.
  5. Food from the water Fishing docks such as [[b:buildings:fishing:dssvfishingdock]] (up to 5 workers, radius 21) or [[b:buildings:fishing:dockfish]]. The Fish n Ships jetties are free to build: [[b:buildings:gathering:fishshipsberrywaterlo]] (berries), [[b:buildings:gathering:fishshipscrabwater]] (crabs), [[b:buildings:gathering:fishshipsplantswaterhi]] (herbs or flax). Also [[b:buildings:food:dsjabjettymolluscfarm]] and [[b:buildings:food:dsjabjettyglasshouse]] (a greenhouse on a jetty platform).
  6. Logs and firewood [[b:buildings:gathering:dsjabjettydriftwoodcollector]] collects floating logs on water, but runs out after 300. [[b:buildings:fuel:dsjabjettyfirewood2x2]] is a 2×2 firewood splitter for water or land.
  7. Services School: [[b:buildings:schools:dockschool]] (45 Logs) or [[b:buildings:schools:nmtschoolstonedock]] (stone, 26 students). Health: [[b:buildings:hospital:nmthospitalstonedock]]. Faith: [[b:buildings:worship:dockchapel]]. Goods: [[b:buildings:market:fishshipsmarketwaterhi]] (a market on a high jetty platform). Trade: canal trading posts such as [[b:buildings:trading_post:nmt30canal4xtradingpostc1]], where merchants arrive by river boat.
