First 10 steps

A build order for a Medium start, using only costs from the wiki. Each step says what it costs so you can check it against what you have.

What a Medium start gives you (Starting Conditions): 5 families, 1,800 food, 200 firewood, 30 clothing, 40 tools, 120 [[i:wikipages:wood:log|Logs]], 60 [[i:wikipages:stone:stone|Stone]], 40 [[i:wikipages:iron:iron|Iron]], 2 crop seeds, 1 orchard seed, and a pre-built Storage Barn + Storage Yard.
  1. Before you start: pick the climate The climate can't be changed later. It sets the growing season, how much firewood people burn, and how often tornadoes come. [CC] Very Mild never gets tornadoes. Compare them in the Season planner (Climate, Disasters).
  2. Build 2 homes next to the Storage Barn [[b:buildings:houses:woodhouse]]: 16 Logs + 8 Stone, 30 labour, houses 5. Two cost 32 Logs + 16 Stone. Put them close to where people work: shorter walks mean more working time (Pathfinding).
  3. Set up firewood [[b:buildings:fuel:cutteryard|Wood Cutter (Cutter Yard)]]: 24 Logs + 8 Stone. It turns 2 Logs into [[i:wikipages:fuel:firewood]]. MegaMod default is 1 worker (max 3) — raise it before winter. You start with 200 firewood.
  4. Food that needs no seeds [[b:buildings:gathering:gatherershelter]]: 30 Logs + 12 Stone, up to 4 workers, gathers wild food within a 35-tile radius. Build it in a forest.
  5. Endless logs [[b:buildings:forester:foresterlodge]]: 32 Logs + 12 Stone, up to 4 Foresters, plants and harvests trees within a 30-tile radius.
    Running total after steps 2–5: 118 Logs and 48 Stone of your 120 Logs and 60 Stone.
  6. Plant your seeds A [[b:buildings:fields:cropfield]] costs nothing to place. In MegaMod a new field starts with 1 worker (max 25); vanilla starts with 4. Set the worker count on every field. Orchard trees need 24 months to mature, so plant the orchard seed early (Seeds). See Farms and food.
  7. Hunting: meat and leather [[b:buildings:hunting:hunterlodge]]: 34 Logs + 12 Stone, radius 34, up to 4 Hunters. Leather is what the Tailor turns into coats. You will need logs from step 5 for this.
  8. Stop logs from filling your storage Set a limit on Logs with the resource limit tool. Otherwise logs can fill the Stockpile and leave no room for firewood, which can lead to freezing (Resource Limits).
  9. Clothing before winter [[b:buildings:clothing:tailor]]: 32 Logs + 48 Stone + 16 Iron. 2 Leather → Leather Coat, or 2 Wool → Wool Coat. You start with 30 clothing. Stone: gathered from boulders or mined at a Quarry ([[i:wikipages:stone:stone]]).
  10. Tools, health and growth Tools wear out (Citizens). [[b:buildings:metalworking:toolmaker]] costs 32 Logs + 55 Stone + 32 Iron.
    Tailor (16 Iron) + Tool Maker (32 Iron) = 48 Iron, more than the 40 you start with. Build the Tailor first, then find an iron source (see Best iron source).
    Then: [[b:buildings:gathering:herbalist]] (30 Logs + 12 Stone) for health, and [[b:buildings:schools:schoolhouse]] (50 Logs + 16 Stone + 16 Iron) so the next generation is educated. Educated citizens get a bonus at every job.

After that

  • Once you have a Town Hall, a Trading Post and a Market, nomads start arriving to join you (Citizens). [[b:buildings:trading_post:tradingpost]]: 82 Logs + 80 Stone + 40 Iron. [[b:buildings:market:market]]: 58 Logs + 62 Stone + 40 Iron.
  • Seeds for new crops are bought from merchants at a Trading Post (Seeds).
  • Use the priority tool when something must be built first (Priority).
