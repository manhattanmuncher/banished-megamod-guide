Farms and food

What the wiki says about getting the most food, turned into a simple layout. The wiki has no data on the ideal field size per farmer, so this guide doesn't give one.

The farm block

The wiki's one rule about efficiency: workers walk home to eat and to warm up in winter, so shorter home-to-work walks mean more time working and more output. Building homes near the workplace fixes long walks (Pathfinding). A farm block puts everything a farmer needs within a few steps:

  1. Center first A [[b:buildings:storage:storagebarn]] (48 Logs + 16 Stone, stores food) and the farmers' homes in the middle.
  2. Fields around it [[b:buildings:fields:cropfield|Crop Fields]] are free to place. Draw them so every side touches the center roads.
  3. Set workers on every field In MegaMod a new Crop Field starts with 1 worker (max 25); vanilla starts with 4 (max 6). An [[b:buildings:fields:orchard]] also starts with 1 in MegaMod (max 25; vanilla 3, max 4). Raise them yourself.
  4. Educate your farmers Every crop page gives two yields: uneducated / educated. For example [[i:wikipages:food:bean]]: 20 / 28. Build a [[b:buildings:schools:schoolhouse]].

Timing with the seasons

  • Crops only grow above freezing, 32 °F (Climate).
  • Growth times run from 2 to 3 months per crop (Seeds). Pick crops that fit your climate in the Season planner.
  • Orchard trees take 24 months to mature, then fruit every 4 months. Plant them early; they take longest to pay off.

Food without seeds or seasons

Several MegaMod patches grow a crop year-round with no input materials, for example [[b:buildings:fields:patchbean]] (1 worker, 3 × 5 tiles) and [[b:buildings:fields:patchbarley]] (Fields). Greenhouses also grow food. The Season planner lists every one, and the cheatsheet ranks them.

Other food sources

  • [[b:buildings:gathering:gatherershelter]], radius 35, wild food.
  • [[b:buildings:hunting:hunterlodge]], radius 34, meat and leather.
  • [[b:buildings:fishing:fishermansdock]], radius 14, fish.
  • A varied diet keeps people healthy: grain, fruit, vegetables and protein. Herbs from a [[b:buildings:gathering:herbalist]] cover gaps (Citizens).
  • Set food limits high enough for your population, or people starve even with productive farms (Resource Limits).
