Trains, ships and wagons

The late-game transport sets in MegaMod: two railway mods, ship and boat building, and carts and wagons. Each one has its own supply chain, so this guide shows what to build first and what each piece needs. Everything comes from the buildings' wiki pages.

Choo Choo railwayExport goods for Coins, spend Coins on a Train Engine. KID RailwayTrain cars that turn coal into food, goods and materials. ShipsHull Components → Wharfship and Logging Barge. Dories and fishing boatsDory + fishing gear → boats that catch up to 300. Free boats and wrecksNo chain needed; good early. Carts and wagonsSmall storage, markets and frontier wagons.

Choo Choo railway (RedKetchup)

Toolbar: Themed Sets → Choo Choo (also RK Toolbar → RK transport → Choo Choo). The Choo Choo set runs on [[i:wikipages:custom6:coins]]: stations sell your surplus for Coins, and the Train Engine spends them.

  1. Build export stations Each costs 24 Logs + 4 Stone and needs 1 Merchant. The Merchant packs a bulk amount of goods with 1 Wood and sells the bundle for Coins (for example 600 Apples).
    • [[b:buildings:trading_post:garemerchant1]]: fruit and other food
    • [[b:buildings:trading_post:garemerchant2]]: textiles and clothing
    • [[b:buildings:trading_post:garemerchant3]]: alcohol, firewood, herbs and tools
    • [[b:buildings:trading_post:garemerchant4]]: bulk building materials
  2. Build a Train Engine [[b:buildings:trading_post:chootrain1]] (180 labour, 24 Logs + 124 Iron): a Train Driver spends Coins to buy a wide range of goods, from food and resources to clothing and crafted items. Model 2 only looks different.
  3. Add wagons as storage along the line: [[b:buildings:storage:choowagon1]] (2,000 food), [[b:buildings:storage:choowagon2]] (2,000 tools, clothing, alcohol, medicine), [[b:buildings:storage:choowagon3]] (5,000 wood, stone, iron, fuel).
  4. Optional extras [[b:buildings:nomads:choowagon4]] is a town hall and a nomad attractor (groups up to 15%); [[b:buildings:houses:choowagon5]] houses 5.
  5. Lay tracks [[b:buildings:roads:choochoo_tracks]]: straight track costs 6 labour + 3 Wood + 2 Iron. The wiki calls the tracks decorative and does not say the other buildings need them, so lay them last.

KID Railway

Toolbar: Themed Sets → Railway (also KID Mods → Themed Mods → Railway). This set runs on [[i:wikipages:coalfuel:coal]]: each train car holds up to 1,000 coal on site, used only for its own production.

Around the cars:

  • [[b:buildings:town_halls:railwaymaster]]: a town hall with Spirit happiness within 35 tiles and resource limits (26 Logs).
  • [[b:buildings:market:railwaystation]]: a market, 5 vendors, 9,000 storage. [[b:buildings:market:railwaycoalbin]] hands out coal fuel.
  • Homes: [[b:buildings:houses:trainhomecar]] (houses 4), [[b:buildings:houses:railwayrooms]] (4 apartments of 3).
  • Storage: [[b:buildings:storage:railwaystorages]] (most goods), [[b:buildings:storage:railwaytickets]] (clothing and textiles), [[b:buildings:storage:railwayminecart]] (coal and iron).
Before building train cars, secure a coal supply: see Best coal source.

Ships: Hull Components

  1. Make the parts Rope, and Linen or Cloth, plus Lumber.
  2. Build a ship yard [[b:buildings:construction:shipyard]] (60 Lumber + 10 Stone + 4 Building Kits) or [[b:buildings:construction:oldshipyard]] (40 Lumber + 40 Stone + 2 Building Kits). Boatmen make 10 Lumber + 1 Rope + 6 Linen (or 6 Cloth) → [[i:wikipages:custom5:hull_components]].
  3. Launch a vessel
    • [[b:buildings:fishing:wharfship]]: 10 Hull Components + 30 Lumber + 30 Stone, 400 labour. 4 Sailors hunt deep water for Whale meat, Blubber, Seal meat or Lobster; you pick which.
    • [[b:buildings:resources:loggingbarge]]: 2 Hull Components + 30 Lumber + 20 Stone. Turns Silver Pfennig into Logs or Hardwood.
Sailors are dangerous: 2.00 deaths per 100 workers per year, the third highest rate in the game (Professions).

Dories and fishing boats

  1. Build dories [[b:buildings:crafting:dorybuilder]] (5 Lumber + 2 Rope → [[i:wikipages:custom5:dory]]), [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoredorybuilder]] (also needs 2 Pine Pitch), or [[b:buildings:crafting:sbboatshop]], which also makes lobster pots, fishing nets and fishing lines.
  2. Make the gear each boat needs 10 of one kind of gear.
  3. Launch NS Inshore boats each costs 1 Dory + 10 gear, has 1 Fisherman, and catches up to 300:
    • [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoreboatherring]] and [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoreboatshrimp]]: 10 Fishing Nets
    • [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoreboatlobster]]: 10 Lobster Pots
    • [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoreboatmackerel]]: 10 Fishing Lines
    • [[b:buildings:fishing:nsinshoreboattuna]]: 10 Fishing Tackle

Free boats and wrecks (no chain)

  • [[b:buildings:mining:otpshipwreck]]: free, on land or water. A worker salvages Logs, Firewood, Iron and Rum.
  • [[b:buildings:mining:sbbarquewreck]]: free; Tools, Firewood, Leather Coats and Herbs, capped at 2,000.
  • [[b:buildings:mining:sbmotorizedwreck]]: free; Coal, Firewood, Stone, Logs and Iron, capped at 2,000.
  • [[b:buildings:gathering:sbsupply]]: free; unloads food supplies, but needs a trader or a jetty to dock.
  • [[b:buildings:trading_post:otplootship]] (36 Logs, on water): pirates scavenge tools, coats, food and sugar.
  • [[b:buildings:trading_post:aptraderjetty]] (8 Logs): merchants arrive by river boat and trade for any goods.
  • Boats as homes: [[b:buildings:houses:kidhouseboat]] (houses 4), [[b:buildings:houses:kidhostelboat]]. More in Life on the Water.
