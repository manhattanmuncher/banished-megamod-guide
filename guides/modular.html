Modular buildings

MegaMod has several kinds of buildings that are meant to be combined: a home paired with a workplace, floors stacked into a tower, top floors added to a house, and buildings that upgrade into a better version. The wiki lists each piece separately but never explains how they fit together. This guide groups them.

What the wiki does not say: it never describes the in-game steps for stacking or upgrading. Where this guide says how pieces combine, it uses only what the wiki states (descriptions, sizes, names). When a step is read from the sizes or names rather than stated, it is marked (not stated on the wiki).

1. Home + workplace pairs (EB Modular Forest)

Each job comes as two buildings: a House where the workers live, and a Shed / Lodge / Yard that “provides the actual work” (wiki). Build them next to each other: workers who live close to their job walk less and work more (Pathfinding).

Toolbar: Themed Sets → Modular Forest Buildings Toolbar. Every House: houses 5, warmth 60 per fuel, 16 Logs + 8 Stone, 4 × 5 tiles.

3. Stone House Addon tower

Toolbar: Themed Sets → Stone House Addon → Tower. A tower is built from a ground floor and numbered floors; each floor also comes in a “(Roof)” model “with a roof cap” (wiki).

  1. Ground floor [[b:buildings:houses:shatowerground]]: 5 × 3 tiles, houses 6.
  2. Floors 2 to 6 [[b:buildings:houses:shatowerfloor2]] … [[b:buildings:houses:shatowerfloor6]]: each 1 × 1, houses 6. The tiny footprint and the numbering suggest each floor goes on top of the one below (not stated on the wiki).
  3. Top it off with the “(Roof)” model of your last floor, for example [[b:buildings:houses:shatowerfloor3roof]].

Every tower piece costs the same: 40 labour + 16 Logs + 16 Stone, warmth 90 per fuel. The same set has tower shops in 5 × 4, each with a roof model: [[b:buildings:bakery:shatowerbaker]], [[b:buildings:butcher:shatowerbutcher]], [[b:buildings:clothing:shatowertailor]], [[b:buildings:metalworking:shatowersmith]], plus [[b:buildings:houses:shatowerhostel]].

6. Modular water network (EB Natural Irrigation)

[[b:buildings:wells:ebnaturalirrigation]] is “a modular water-distribution network” (wiki):

  1. Raise water with a Water Lift (river water; 32 labour, 28 Wood, 4 Iron) or a Water Well Lift (ground water; 24 labour, 18 Wood).
  2. Carry it with channel, gutter, corner, curve, culvert, bridge and gate pieces (a straight channel: 3 × 1, 10 labour, 1 Wood).
  3. End at a Water Reservoir. Ghosted (no-footprint) versions of the pieces exist too.
