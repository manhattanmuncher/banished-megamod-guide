Tools in the game

These are toolbar tools rather than keys, but they are the controls the wiki explains:

  • Priority tool: drag over an area to make workers build or work there first (Priority).
  • Resource limit tool: cap how much of a resource is stored (Resource Limits).
  • Path tool: click a home or workplace to see the routes its people walk (Pathfinding).
Neither wiki lists other game hotkeys (pausing, game speed, toolbar shortcuts), so they aren't included here.
