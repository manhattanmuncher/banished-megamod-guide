How to read a wiki page

  • xBAN = the vanilla Banished value. xMOD = the value in MegaMod. When a building shows both, MegaMod is what you play.
  • Where to Find shows the toolbar path in the game, icon by icon. Many buildings sit under more than one toolbar.
  • Work Required in the build cost is labour; the rest is materials.
  • Links with a dotted underline are pages the wiki doesn't have yet.

Guides and tools only use what the wiki states. Where the wiki has no data, they say so. Icons that were broken on the wiki were replaced with the original game art.
