Death and cemeteries

What kills citizens according to the wiki, how to prevent it, and how to place cemeteries. The wiki gives no burial capacity or happiness radius for most cemeteries, so the placement advice below sticks to what it does state.

Why death matters

Deaths lower happiness; births and marriages raise it. Unhappy citizens idle more (Citizens). Cemeteries are one of the buildings that raise happiness.

Picking a cemetery

Almost every cemetery is a player-drawn area: you drag it to the size you want and pay per tile. Toolbar: Town Services → Cemeteries (or Colonial Charter → Town Services → Worship and Remembrance).

The one cemetery with numbers: [[b:buildings:cemeteries:nvburialground]] (Native Village). Free to build, 7×7 up to 20×20 tiles, 4 Gravediggers bury the dead, and it gives Spirit happiness within 30 tiles.

Placing it well

  1. Keep homes within reach of the happiness. The Burial Ground's Spirit happiness reaches 30 tiles, so put it in the middle of a housing area, not at the edge of the map.
  2. Pair it with worship. Colonial Charter groups cemeteries and chapels in one menu, Worship and Remembrance. A chapel such as the [[b:buildings:worship:chapel]] also gives Spirit happiness within 35 tiles, so one spot can cover both.
  3. Start small, grow it later. Areas are drawn and paid per tile, so a free or 1-labour cemetery costs almost nothing to start.
  4. Decorate for free. [[b:buildings:cemeteries:ghostgravestone]] (1 labour + 1 Stone; press F to cycle its look), [[b:buildings:cemeteries:gfcrypt01]], [[b:buildings:cemeteries:gfcrypt02]] and tombstones are 1 × 1 pieces.

Not on the wiki: how many graves fit per tile, and the happiness radius of ordinary cemeteries. Check a cemetery's page if this matters to you.
