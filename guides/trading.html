Trading and money

How to trade with merchants, and the three currencies MegaMod adds: Coins, Silver Pfennig / Gold Guilder, and Ale. From the wiki's Trading page and the buildings' own pages.

Trading with merchants

  1. Build a Trading Post on a river or lake Merchants arrive by boat, so a post on a lake with no river access never gets visitors. [[b:buildings:trading_post:tradingpost]]: 82 Logs + 80 Stone + 40 Iron. A cheap alternative: [[b:buildings:trading_post:aptraderjetty]] (8 Logs).
  2. Stock the post Click it and set a desired amount per item; Traders carry goods between town storage and the post, and bring back surplus.
  3. Trade When a merchant arrives, press Trade. Left: what the merchant sells. Right: what your post holds. What you offer must be worth at least what you buy. Every item page shows its trade value.
  4. Automate it In the Purchase tab, set buys to run when a merchant arrives or before they leave, with priorities.
  5. Order what you need The Order tab requests goods from the next merchant (once or every time). Ordered items cost more than the merchant's normal stock.
Merchants are where you get new crop seeds, orchard seeds and livestock. Trading Posts also give Goods happiness (see Happiness).

Coins (Choo Choo)

Made by the Choo Choo export stations, where a Merchant packs bulk goods with 1 Wood and sells them for Coins, for example [[b:buildings:trading_post:garemerchant1]]. Spent by the [[b:buildings:trading_post:chootrain1]] to buy goods. Full chain in Trains, ships and wagons.

Ale (pirates)

[[b:buildings:trading_post:smugglerscave]]: pirates trade [[i:wikipages:alcohol:ale]] for goods. Cheap items cost 1–7 Ale; livestock costs 66 Ale per animal. Make Ale at an Ale House (30 Apples → Ale) or an Abbey.

Every item's trade value is on its page and in its hover card. Look up any resource in the Resource lookup.
