Surviving winter

Three things keep people warm: a warm home, firewood in it, and clothing.

  1. Know how cold your climate gets Each climate has a winter low, from 80 °F (Perfect) down to −50 °F (Siberian). Temperature sets how much firewood people burn. In warm climates citizens only need clothing (Climate, Season planner).
  2. Keep firewood flowing A home is warm when it has enough firewood or coal (Citizens). The vanilla [[b:buildings:fuel:cutteryard|Wood Cutter]] uses 2 Logs per Firewood recipe; MegaMod has faster options, see Best firewood maker. In MegaMod the Wood Cutter starts with 1 worker (max 3).
  3. Build warmer houses Each house lists Fuel Per Resource: warmth per fuel item, higher means less firewood. [[b:buildings:houses:woodhouse]]: 60. [[b:buildings:houses:stonehouse]]: 90 (costs 24 Logs + 40 Stone + 10 Iron). The warmest houses on the wiki are in the cheatsheet.
  4. Make clothing Clothing lets people stay outside longer in the cold. The [[b:buildings:clothing:tailor]] makes a coat from 2 Leather or 2 Wool. Leather comes from hunters; wool from sheep in a pasture (Livestock).
  5. Don't let logs crowd out firewood If logs fill your stockpile there's no room for firewood or coal, and people can freeze. Set a limit on Logs (Resource Limits).
Stone houses are also safer from fire. Wood houses catch fire about every 20 years at population 100 and spread it 50% of the time; stone houses every 30 years with 33% spread (Disasters).
